# Three-Sentence Cover Letter Prompt

Use this when generating a quick, punchy cover letter for a job application. The intended style is direct: who you are, what problem you can solve, how your CV supports it, and a short call to action.

```text
Alright, next I would want a quick punchy cover letter like this. I was taught by the career coach that once again, hiring managers don't have time to read through paragraphs of text and so we need to be straight to the point, who you are, what problem you can solve and how, directing back to your CV for more details. So she came up with this 3 sentence cover letter. Below is her template:

----
The truth is... you only need 3 sentences

Sentence 1 = Trigger
Sentence 2 = Highlight suitability
Sentence 3 = Call to Action

Grab your templates below
--

The Three Sentence Cover Letter:
This cover letter should be used when you meet the requirements for the position advertised.

Hi [First Name],

I noticed you're hiring for [insert job title] and I feel I would be a good fit.

I have [Insert Number] years of experience in [Detail the specific Skill or Tasks required for the position] as highlighted in the professional summary of my resume.

I'd welcome the opportunity to speak if you feel I would be a strong candidate for this role or others within the organization.

All the best,
Name

--

Here's my former cover letter. Might not be good but I tried to also state that I understand the specific problem that she is facing and that I can solve it but feel free to frame your cover letter as you like:

Dear Hiring Manager,
I noticed you're hiring for a Commercial Analyst in your Financial Services team, and I feel I would be a strong fit.
I have 4+ years of experience turning customer, behavioural, and operational data into commercial insight, with recent work focused on personalised offers, loyalty targeting, customer segmentation, proposition analysis, and evidence-led business recommendations.
I'm also proficient in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, SQL, Python, customer behaviour analysis, offer performance analysis, trend analysis, and stakeholder-ready reporting, as highlighted in my CV.
I know you are not looking for just any analyst, but for someone who can support the Financial Services loyalty agenda by using insight and analysis to shape propositions, improve personalised customer offers, understand customer behaviour, and help drive spend, revenue, and stronger customer journeys.
So, I would love to see how the customer analytics and commercial insight experience I've built so far could support M&S Financial Services, your loyalty programme, and your wider commercial teams.
I'd welcome the opportunity to speak if you feel I would be a strong candidate for this role or others within the organisation.
All the best,
Esosa Orumwese
```

## Working Instruction

When using this prompt, produce a short cover letter that keeps the three-sentence structure unless the user explicitly asks for a fuller version:

```text
Hi [First Name / Hiring Manager],

I noticed you're hiring for [job title], and I feel I would be a strong fit because [specific job problem from the ad].

I have [number] years of experience in [specific role-relevant evidence], with my CV showing [2-3 strongest proof points tied to the job].

I'd welcome the opportunity to speak if you feel I would be a strong candidate for this role or others within the organisation.

All the best,
Esosa Orumwese
```
