# Three-Sentence Cover Letter Prompt

Use this when generating a quick, punchy cover letter for a job application. The intended style is direct: who you are, what problem you can solve, how your CV supports it, and a short call to action.

```text
Alright, next I would want a quick punchy cover letter like this. I was taught by the career coach that once again, hiring managers don't have time to read through paragraphs of text and so we need to be straight to the point, who you are, what problem you can solve and how, directing back to your CV for more details. So she came up with this 3 sentence cover letter. Below is her template:

----
The truth is... you only need 3 sentences

Sentence 1 = Trigger
Sentence 2 = Highlight suitability
Sentence 3 = Call to Action

Grab your templates below
--

The Three Sentence Cover Letter:
This cover letter should be used when you meet the requirements for the position advertised.

Hi [First Name],

I noticed you're hiring for [insert job title] and I feel I would be a good fit.

I have [Insert Number] years of experience in [Detail the specific Skill or Tasks required for the position] as highlighted in the professional summary of my resume.

I'd welcome the opportunity to speak if you feel I would be a strong candidate for this role or others within the organization.

All the best,
Name

--

Here's my former cover letter. Might not be good but I tried to also state that I understand the specific problem that she is facing and that I can solve it but feel free to frame your cover letter as you like:

Hi Hiring Manager,
I noticed you're hiring for a Junior Data and Information Analyst, and I feel I would be a good fit.
I have hands-on experience improving data quality, metadata clarity and responsible data use across shared reporting outputs, as highlighted in the professional summary of my resume.
I know you're looking for someone who can support open and trusted data, improve governance, and help people understand and use data responsibly, so I’d welcome the opportunity to speak if you feel I would be a strong candidate for this role or others within the organisation.
All the best,
Esosa Orumwese
```
