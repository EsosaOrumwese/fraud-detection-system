## 1. Requirement Being Targeted

The requirement being targeted here is the South Tyneside and Sunderland NHS Foundation Trust `Data Analyst` expectation around:
- handling complex statistical and analytical queries
- investigating whether analyses are robust
- investigating data anomalies identified during analysis
- instigating corrective action as required
- validating data quality
- resolving anomalies
- identifying data-quality issues and source-system limitations
- performing cleansing and consistency checks
- applying data-governance requirements, anonymisation protocols, and quality-assurance techniques
- reviewing and updating analysis-production methods and documentation following changes to data, processing, or requirements

This maps directly to the South Tyneside candidate-profile requirement that the analyst should be able to sound like someone who:
- can assess the robustness of BI outputs rather than only produce them
- can investigate anomalies and trace likely causes
- can apply corrective action and preventative thinking
- can keep reporting outputs valid, governed, and fit for purpose in a healthcare environment

This note exists because that requirement is easy to flatten into the vague statement:
`good at data quality`

That is not the real burden.

The employer is asking for something sharper:
- anomaly investigation
- robustness checking
- corrective action
- trusted-output control
- governed BI validity

- careful not to imply a full data-quality office, warehouse administration estate, or whole-Trust governance function where this slice only proves one bounded anomaly-and-control pack
