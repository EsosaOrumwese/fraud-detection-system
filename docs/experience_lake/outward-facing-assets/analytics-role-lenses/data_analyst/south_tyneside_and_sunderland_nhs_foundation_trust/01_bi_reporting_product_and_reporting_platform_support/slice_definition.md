## 1. Requirement Being Targeted

The requirement being targeted here is the South Tyneside and Sunderland NHS Foundation Trust `Data Analyst` expectation around:
- providing reports, dashboards, datasets, and visual management-information products
- supporting the development and promotion of the Trust data warehouse
- supporting the development and promotion of the strategic reporting platform
- promoting use of that strategic reporting platform as the main information source throughout the Trust
- carrying out the design, documentation, communication, development, implementation, processing, and maintenance of requirements relevant to defined reporting areas

This maps directly to the South Tyneside candidate-profile requirement that the analyst should be able to sound like someone who:
- can build and maintain reporting products that services actually use
- can support a wider reporting ecosystem rather than isolated one-off reports
- can help make a trusted reporting environment the main management-information source
- can shape reporting requirements and flows in a BI team setting

This note exists because that requirement is easy to flatten into the vague statement:
`good at dashboards and BI`

That is not the real burden.

The employer is asking for something sharper:
- reporting-product delivery
- reporting-platform and data-warehouse support
- trusted-source posture
- BI requirements and data-flow maintenance

## 2. The Analogue On The Current Platform

The current platform does not contain a literal South Tyneside strategic reporting platform, live Trust data warehouse, or a full enterprise BI estate, so the slice has to use an honest analogue.

The closest truthful analogue is:
- one governed prepared reporting base that can behave like a BI-product-ready source
- one dashboard-style or management-information output built from that base
- one reporting-platform-support surface that makes trusted-source use explicit
- one service-usable management-information reading that explains why the same pack should be preferred for recurring use

So for this slice:
- `reports, dashboards, datasets, and visualisation service` maps to `one bounded BI reporting-product pack with more than one product surface built from the same governed logic`
- `support the Trust data warehouse` maps to `one explicit prepared base or reporting-ready source layer rather than raw-output-only proof`
- `support and promote the strategic reporting platform as the main information source` maps to `one trusted-source support reading and one reporting-platform-support surface`
- `design, documentation, communication, development, implementation, processing, and maintenance of requirements relevant to reporting areas` maps to `one explicit reporting-product and reporting-support chain rather than isolated output production`

The claim must therefore remain:
- prepared-reporting-base shaped
- reporting-product shaped
- trusted-source shaped
- reporting-platform-support shaped
- service-usable management-information shaped
- careful not to imply a full warehouse estate, live strategic reporting-platform ownership, or enterprise BI architecture where this slice only proves one bounded reporting-product and support pack

Default first-pass posture:
- prepared reporting base first
- dashboard or management-information surface second
- reporting-platform-support surface third
- trusted-source and service-usable reading fourth
- the trusted-source proof should be fixed from the inherited reporting-product logic before any stronger platform rhetoric is allowed
- stronger warehouse or platform-ownership language only if the bounded evidence genuinely supports it
