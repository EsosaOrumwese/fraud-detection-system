## 8. Workflow-Boundary Strategy

The trusted-source and platform side has to stay honest.

The first-pass strategy should answer:
- what exactly counts as the prepared reporting base?
- why is it acceptable as a BI reporting-product analogue?
- what does it add to the management-information side?
- why does that still stop short of a live warehouse or strategic-platform claim?

Allowed posture:
- prepared-base reuse
- bounded reporting-platform support
- compact management-information readiness tied to the same reporting question

Disallowed posture:
- claiming direct live warehouse ownership where the slice only uses bounded preparation logic
- implying ownership of the strategic reporting platform estate

Decision rule:
- every retained preparation or support element must earn its place in either:
  - prepared reporting base
  - management-information output
  - reporting-platform-support output
  - trusted-source explanation
- if a preparation or support element does not support one of those purposes, drop it
