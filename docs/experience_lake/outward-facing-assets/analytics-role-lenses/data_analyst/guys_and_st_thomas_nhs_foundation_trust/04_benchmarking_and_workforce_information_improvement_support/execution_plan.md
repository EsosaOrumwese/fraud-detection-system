- why does the slice stop at bounded comparator context rather than a broader benchmark-programme claim?

Allowed posture:
- one bounded comparator context
- one compact benchmark-aware reading

Disallowed posture:

- asserting direct control of a workforce-systems strategy environment
- importing generic strategy language as fake proof

Decision rule:
- every retained comparator element must earn its place in either:
  - benchmarking
  - comparator-aware interpretation
  - future reporting-development support
  - boundary-safe evidence of workforce-information-improvement direction
