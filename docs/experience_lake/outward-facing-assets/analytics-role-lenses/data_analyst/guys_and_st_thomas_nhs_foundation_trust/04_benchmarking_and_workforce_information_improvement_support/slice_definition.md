## 1. Requirement Being Targeted

The requirement being targeted here is the Guy's and St Thomas' `Senior Data Analyst` expectation around:
- carrying out benchmarking and external research and analysis of data
- putting Trust information into context in relation to other Trusts and sectors
- working closely with relevant stakeholders to keep them informed of developments to workforce information
- collating and documenting feedback, issues, and comments from users and managers
- using feedback to investigate and develop suggestions for future workforce-information reporting
- keeping abreast of new technology and developments around workforce information and reporting
- seeking ideas for improvements to reporting strategies and systems
- contributing to the formulation of overall strategies around workforce systems and information
- investigating innovative approaches to delivering the service provided by the team
- exploring examples such as electronic forms, a new reporting platform, and automating regular reports
- supporting the systems team where necessary

This maps directly to the candidate-profile requirement that the analyst should be able to sound like someone who:
- can put Trust workforce information into context rather than reporting it in isolation
- can use benchmark and external evidence to strengthen interpretation and recommendations
- can turn feedback and developments into better workforce-information reporting
- can contribute to workforce-information and reporting-system improvement without overstating full systems ownership

This note exists because that requirement is easy to flatten into the vague statement:
`good at benchmarking and systems improvement`

That is not the real burden.

The employer is asking for something sharper:
- benchmarking and external-context analysis
- feedback-led improvement of workforce-information reporting
- technology-aware reporting and systems improvement support
- bounded contribution to workforce-information strategy
