## 1. Requirement Being Targeted

The requirement being targeted here is the Guy's and St Thomas' `Senior Data Analyst` expectation around:
- leading accurate and timely workforce-intelligence reporting
- creating and maintaining `EDI` dashboards
- supporting or delivering statutory equality-reporting outputs such as `WRES`, `WDES`, and `Gender Pay Gap`
- preparing data and analysis for boards, forums, and senior stakeholders
- presenting workforce information in user-friendly formats that support decision-making

This maps directly to the candidate-profile requirement that the analyst should be able to sound like someone who:
- can own or lead recurring workforce-information delivery rather than only contribute to isolated reports
- can combine dashboard and statutory-reporting discipline
- can package people-intelligence outputs safely enough for high-accountability NHS use
- can keep the reporting lane usable for boards, senior forums, and operational stakeholders

This note exists because that requirement is easy to flatten into the vague statement:
`good at workforce reporting`

That is not the real burden.

The employer is asking for something sharper:
- governed workforce-intelligence delivery
- `EDI` dashboard ownership or equivalent dashboard leadership
- statutory or compliance-style reporting outputs
- senior-facing readability from sensitive workforce data

## 2. The Analogue On The Current Platform

The current platform does not contain a literal `ESR`, `Oracle HRMS`, `Discoverer`, `WRES`, `WDES`, or `Gender Pay Gap` reporting estate, so the slice has to use an honest analogue.

The closest truthful analogue is:
- one governed workforce-style reporting lane over sensitive organisational data
- one `EDI` dashboard-style summary surface
- one statutory-return-style or compliance-style reporting surface
- one board-ready or senior-forum-ready interpretation note
- one release-safe reporting pack over the same bounded equality-style focus question

So for this slice:
- `lead accurate and timely workforce intelligence` maps to `produce one governed recurring workforce-intelligence-style reporting pack`
- `create and maintain EDI dashboards` maps to `materialise one bounded equality-style dashboard surface`
- `deliver WRES/WDES/Gender Pay Gap style returns` maps to `materialise one statutory-reporting-style summary from the same governed base`
- `prepare data and analysis for Boards and forums` maps to `write one board-ready interpretation from the same pack`

The claim must therefore remain:
- workforce-intelligence shaped
- governed-reporting shaped
- dashboard-and-statutory-reporting shaped
- platform-truth bounded
- careful not to imply literal workforce-system administration, live statutory-submission authority, or the whole Trust reporting function where this slice only proves one bounded workforce-reporting pack

Default first-pass posture:
- governed workforce reporting first
- `EDI` dashboard-style surface second
- statutory-return-style surface third
- board-ready written reading fourth
- stronger live-system language only if the bounded evidence genuinely earns it
