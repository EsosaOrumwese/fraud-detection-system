## 1. Requirement Being Targeted

The requirement being targeted here is the Guy's and St Thomas' `Senior Data Analyst` expectation around:
- supporting the Associate Director of `EDI` in co-ordinating projects relating to workforce information and `ESR`
- establishing and measuring the impact of programmes of work
- planning defined projects from initiation through to completion
- managing defined projects from initiation through to completion
- implementing defined projects, especially projects related to workforce intelligence such as workforce planning and data-quality improvement
- carrying out resource analysis for projects and major work within the team
- using project initiation documents, risk registers, and highlight reports where appropriate
- managing major external information returns or internal reports such as `WRES`, `WDES`, and `Gender Pay report` as distinct projects
- ensuring accurate and timely delivery of those projects and producing progress updates for senior stakeholders

This maps directly to the candidate-profile requirement that the analyst should be able to sound like someone who:
- can manage projects as well as reports
- can coordinate defined workforce-information work rather than only produce the analytics inside it
- can use bounded project controls and progress tracking
- can state an impact reading for programmes of work rather than only claim delivery activity

This note exists because that requirement is easy to flatten into the vague statement:
`good at project management`

That is not the real burden.

The employer is asking for something sharper:
- workforce-information project coordination
- reporting-cycle management as a project discipline
- bounded use of initiation, risk, and progress-control surfaces
- impact measurement of programmes of work
