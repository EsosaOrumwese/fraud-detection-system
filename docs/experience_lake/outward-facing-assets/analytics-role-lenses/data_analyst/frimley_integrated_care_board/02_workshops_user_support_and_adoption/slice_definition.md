## 1. Requirement Being Targeted

The requirement being targeted here is the NHS Frimley Integrated Care Board `Associate Data Analyst - Connected Care` expectation around:
- supporting or delivering workshops, demonstrations, and training on analytical products
- engaging with stakeholders to understand information and usability needs
- troubleshooting issues with dashboards, tools, or data products
- giving practical advice and hands-on support
- improving adoption by making the products easier to use, explain, and trust

This maps directly to the Frimley candidate-profile requirement that the analyst should be able to sound like someone who:
- can support people in using analytical products confidently
- can demonstrate and explain the products in practical sessions
- can respond to usability friction rather than building in isolation
- can iterate the product or its guidance based on stakeholder experience

This note exists because that requirement is easy to flatten into the vague statement:
`good with stakeholders`

That is not the real burden.

The employer is asking for something sharper:
- workshop and demonstration support
- product adoption and confidence-building
- user-facing troubleshooting and guidance
- usability improvement informed by direct engagement

- one adoption note showing how the product becomes easier to use after that support layer is added

So for this slice:

The claim must therefore remain:

- usability shaped
- careful not to imply a full training estate, a staffed support function, or broad organisational change ownership where this slice only proves one bounded adoption-and-support layer over one product pack
