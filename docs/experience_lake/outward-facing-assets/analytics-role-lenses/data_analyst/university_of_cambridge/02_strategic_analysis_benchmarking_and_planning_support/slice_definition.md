## 1. Requirement Being Targeted

The requirement being targeted here is the University of Cambridge `Research and Data Analyst` expectation around:
- producing critical analysis to support planning, strategy, and wider decision-making
- analysing large, complex datasets and national or external data sources to support planning and benchmarking
- identifying, interpreting, and using comparator or benchmark datasets
- benchmarking against peer institutions or comparable contexts
- interpreting findings so they can inform strategy, policy, and service development
- advising stakeholders on options for using data and analysis in planning and policy work

This maps directly to the Cambridge candidate-profile requirement that the analyst should be able to sound like someone who:
- can move beyond status reporting into comparative strategic interpretation
- can use internal and external reference points together
- can position current performance or pressure against relevant comparator context
- can turn that comparison into planning-support or policy-support advice

This note exists because that requirement is easy to flatten into the vague statement:
`good at strategic analysis and benchmarking`

That is not the real burden.

The employer is asking for something sharper:
- strategic comparative analysis
- benchmark-aware interpretation
- planning-support reading
- one clearer option or implication for policy, strategy, or service development
