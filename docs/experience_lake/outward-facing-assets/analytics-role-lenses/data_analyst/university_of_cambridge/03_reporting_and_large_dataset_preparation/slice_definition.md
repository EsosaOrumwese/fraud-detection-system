## 1. Requirement Being Targeted

The requirement being targeted here is the University of Cambridge `Research and Data Analyst` expectation around:
- preparing, manipulating, validating, normalising, combining, and organising large and diverse datasets
- improving extraction and reporting workflows
- producing reports, dashboards, and accessible outputs from prepared analytical bases
- making data fit for purpose for routine decision-support and institutional use

This maps directly to the Cambridge candidate-profile requirement that the analyst should be able to sound like someone who:
- can handle large and complex datasets rather than only consume finished outputs
- can turn diverse sources into a reliable prepared analytical base
- can package that base into usable reports and dashboards
- can improve the readiness and usability of reporting outputs for non-specialist users

This note exists because that requirement is easy to flatten into the vague statement:
`good at reporting and data prep`

That is not the real burden.

The employer is asking for something sharper:
- large-dataset preparation
- combined and normalised reporting base
- usable reporting or dashboard surface
- workflow-readiness and reporting usability support
