## 1. Requirement Being Targeted

The requirement being targeted here is the University of Cambridge `Research and Data Analyst` expectation around:
- writing high-quality analysis and reports
- producing clear recommendations for stakeholders with different levels of knowledge
- presenting findings clearly to non-specialist audiences
- presenting findings to committees and working groups
- turning complex findings into insight that non-data specialists can understand and use

This maps directly to the Cambridge candidate-profile requirement that the analyst should be able to sound like someone who:
- can convert complex analytical work into clear written outputs
- can shape findings for committees, working groups, and governance-style audiences
- can give recommendation-led interpretation rather than just describing the data
- can preserve analytical integrity while making the output readable for non-specialists

This note exists because that requirement is easy to flatten into the vague statement:
`good at communicating analysis`

That is not the real burden.

The employer is asking for something sharper:
- committee-ready analysis
- clear recommendation writing
- non-specialist translation
- one decision-useful briefing surface
