## 1. Requirement Being Targeted

The requirement being targeted here is the University of Cambridge `Research and Data Analyst` expectation around:
- conducting robust qualitative and quantitative research
- choosing suitable research and analysis methodologies
- using mixed methods such as surveys, interviews, focus groups, and quantitative evidence
- evaluating interventions or programmes
- showing effectiveness or impact in a defensible way
- supporting accountability and benefits analysis through evaluation evidence

This maps directly to the Cambridge candidate-profile requirement that the analyst should be able to sound like someone who:
- can combine qualitative and quantitative evidence
- can choose and justify suitable methods
- can evaluate whether an intervention or programme worked
- can produce evidence strong enough to inform accountability and future decisions

This note exists because that requirement is easy to flatten into the vague statement:
`good at research and evaluation`

That is not the real burden.

The employer is asking for something sharper:
- mixed-method evaluation
- defensible effectiveness reading
- accountability-support evidence
- bounded benefits-style interpretation

## 2. The Analogue On The Current Platform

The current platform does not contain a literal Cambridge education-services evaluation estate, real surveys or focus groups, or an academic intervention-study environment, so the slice has to use an honest analogue.

The closest truthful analogue is:
- one compact quantitative effect surface already available from governed analytical outputs
- one bounded qualitative or narrative-style evidence surface built from coded findings, action-language, or support-language material already present on the platform
- one combined effectiveness reading that uses both evidence types together
- one accountability or benefits-support note stating what the combined evaluation evidence is strong enough to support

So for this slice:
- `qualitative and quantitative research capability` maps to `one mixed-method-style evaluation pack with one quantitative surface and one qualitative-style support surface`
- `support survey writing, interviews, focus groups, or similar methods` maps to `one bounded narrative or coded-evidence analogue rather than a fake live research fieldwork claim`
- `evaluate interventions and show effectiveness` maps to `one explicit intervention-effectiveness or support-effectiveness reading`
- `support accountability and benefits analysis` maps to `one bounded interpretation note about what decisions or accountability claims the evidence can support`

The claim must therefore remain:
- mixed-method shaped
- evaluation shaped
- effectiveness shaped
- careful not to imply a full Cambridge research programme, real live fieldwork estate, or strong causal-certainty claim where this slice only proves one bounded mixed-method evaluation pack
