## 4. Evaluation Scope Gate

Before building either the quantitative evaluation output or the qualitative-style support output, run a bounded scope gate.

The first profiling pass must answer:
- which compact inherited output best represents the quantitative effect or change-reading side?
- what is the single bounded intervention or support question for the slice?
- which inherited note-like or coded-evidence artefact best represents the qualitative-style side?
- can both evidence surfaces be aligned honestly without pretending they came from a live Cambridge fieldwork estate?
- does the resulting pack remain compact enough to act as a bounded proof object?

Required checks:
- continuity of the quantitative effect or change-reading surface
- continuity of the bounded focus question
- ability to define one compact qualitative-style support surface
- ability to produce one combined effectiveness reading without opening a second unrelated analytical question

Those checks should be implemented as:
- aggregate-only or compact-output probes
- inherited compact outputs and selected note-like artefacts where available
- no broad row materialisation into memory

Decision rule:
- if the inherited quantitative and note-like surfaces are explicit enough, proceed with the bounded mixed-method pack
- if not, add one compact coded support layer rather than broadening the query footprint

## 8. Qualitative-Boundary Strategy

The qualitative side has to stay honest.

The first-pass qualitative strategy should answer:
- what exactly counts as the qualitative-style surface?
- why is it acceptable as a qualitative analogue?
- what does it add to the quantitative evaluation side?
- why does that still stop short of a real live fieldwork claim?

Allowed qualitative posture:
- coded issue-like, support-like, finding-like, or action-language analytical surfaces
- compact narrative-style evidence tied to the same bounded question

Disallowed qualitative posture:
- claiming real surveys, focus groups, or interviews where the slice only uses bounded analytical notes or coded language surfaces

Decision rule:
- every retained qualitative-style element must earn its place in either:
  - combined effectiveness interpretation
  - accountability-facing context
  - boundary-safe evidence of mixed-method evaluation
- if a qualitative-style element does not support one of those purposes, drop it

## 9. Effectiveness And Accountability Strategy

The effectiveness reading also has to stay honest.

The first-pass effectiveness strategy should answer:
- what is the bounded intervention or support effect being assessed?
- what evidence supports an effectiveness reading rather than only a descriptive pattern?
- what can be said about accountability or benefits-style value without drifting into quantified institutional-benefit claims?
- where should causal language stop?

Allowed effectiveness posture:
- support-effectiveness
- intervention-effectiveness
- bounded evidence of what appears to have worked
- accountability-support and future-decision support

Disallowed effectiveness posture:
- strong causal proof beyond the compact evidence
- quantified university-wide benefits ownership
- broad programme-level effectiveness claims beyond the bounded question
