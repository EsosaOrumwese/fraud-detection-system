## 1. Requirement Being Targeted

The requirement being targeted here is the Kettering `UHN Patient Safety Data Analyst` expectation around:
- playing a central part in the implementation, monitoring, and continual enhancement of `PSIRF`
- working with the `Patient Safety Team`, clinical leads, and incident investigators throughout the `PSIRF` response cycle
- providing timely and robust data support for identification of patient-safety incidents
- providing timely and robust data support for investigation of patient-safety incidents
- providing timely and robust data support for learning from patient-safety incidents
- supporting thematic reviews and deep-dives into patient-safety issues
- contributing to development of local patient-safety learning responses
- facilitating organisation-wide understanding of data insights within the `PSIRF` framework

This maps directly to the candidate-profile requirement that the analyst should be able to sound like someone who:
- understands that patient-safety analytics is part of the response and learning process, not just the reporting process
- can support incident review, thematic review, and learning responses with timely data
- can shape analytical outputs for investigators, clinical leads, and patient-safety teams
- can keep the incident-learning lane governed, bounded, and decision-useful

This note exists because that requirement is easy to flatten into the vague statement:
`good at patient-safety reporting`

That is not the real burden.

The employer is asking for something sharper:
- incident identification support
- investigation and thematic-review support
- learning-cycle analytics
- `PSIRF`-style analytical contribution

The current platform does not contain a literal live `PSIRF` programme, acute patient-safety investigation service, or full hospital clinical-governance function, so the slice has to use an honest analogue.

The closest truthful analogue is:
- one governed incident-and-case-style analytical lane
- one bounded incident-support summary

- one learning-response or learning-cycle note tied to the same evidence

So for this slice:
- `support identification of patient safety incidents` maps to `materialise one bounded incident-support surface from a governed incident-and-case lane`
- `support investigations and thematic reviews` maps to `materialise one thematic-review or deep-dive output over the same lane`
- `support learning from incidents` maps to `state one bounded learning-response reading rather than a fake patient-safety programme`
- `facilitate organisation-wide understanding` maps to `write one stakeholder-safe learning note that keeps the analytical meaning explicit`

The claim must therefore remain:
- patient-safety shaped
- incident-response shaped
- learning-cycle shaped
- careful not to imply literal `PSIRF` ownership, incident-investigation ownership, or whole-Trust clinical-governance ownership where this slice only proves one bounded incident-response analytics pack

Default first-pass posture:
- incident-support summary first
- incident-support evidence summary second
- thematic-review surface third
- learning-response reading fourth
- release-safe incident-learning note fifth
- stronger programme or governance language only if the bounded evidence genuinely supports it
