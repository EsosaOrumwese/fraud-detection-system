## 1. Requirement Being Targeted

The requirement being targeted here is the Kettering `UHN Patient Safety Data Analyst` expectation around:
- systematically collecting and analysing data on compliance with `sepsis`, `AKI`, and `VTE` targets
- clearly evidencing progress against those compliance measures in dashboards, reports, and analytical outputs
- measuring the impact of patient-safety and quality-improvement interventions
- informing prioritisation and targeted action through data
- helping continuous-improvement activity remain evidence-based over time

This maps directly to the candidate-profile requirement that the analyst should be able to sound like someone who:
- can monitor named clinical-compliance targets rather than just report generic activity
- can evaluate whether interventions are working rather than only showing status
- can keep improvement work evidence-based and accountable
- can turn compliance and intervention evidence into practical prioritisation support

This note exists because that requirement is easy to flatten into the vague statement:
`good at patient-safety dashboard reporting`

That is not the real burden.

The employer is asking for something sharper:
- compliance-target monitoring
- explicit evidencing of `sepsis`, `AKI`, and `VTE` progress
- intervention-effectiveness judgement
- prioritisation and continuous-improvement support
