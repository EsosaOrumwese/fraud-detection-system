## 1. Requirement Being Targeted

The requirement being targeted here is the Kettering `UHN Patient Safety Data Analyst` expectation around:
- performing advanced statistical analyses
- identifying emerging risks
- proactively identifying patterns and risks
- supporting timely and targeted interventions through early pattern and risk detection
- presenting complex analyses in formats including `SPC` charts

This maps directly to the candidate-profile requirement that the analyst should be able to sound like someone who:
- can perform robust advanced analysis across multiple patient-safety sources
- can identify risks and trends early rather than only describing settled issues
- can use `SPC` or similar statistical-improvement methods in a real service-support context
- can turn complex analytical findings into controlled patient-safety action without pretending to own a predictive service

This note exists because that requirement is easy to flatten into the vague statement:
`good at advanced analysis`

That is not the real burden.

The employer is asking for something sharper:
- early pattern and emerging-risk detection
- advanced analytical judgement
- `SPC`-style or statistical-improvement support
- timely targeting of patient-safety intervention
