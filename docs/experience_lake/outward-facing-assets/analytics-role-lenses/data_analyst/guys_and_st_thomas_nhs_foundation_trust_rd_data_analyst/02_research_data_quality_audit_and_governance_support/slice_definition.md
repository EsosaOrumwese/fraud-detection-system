## 1. Requirement Being Targeted

The requirement being targeted here is the Guy's and St Thomas' `R&D Data Analyst` expectation around:
- maintaining data quality in the `EDGE` research-management-system environment
- ensuring required recruitment data can flow between `CPMS` and `EDGE`
- performing quality control to ensure consistency and accuracy
- performing data analysis and auditing to ensure consistency and accuracy
- working with relevant staff to resolve data-quality issues affecting reports
- making recommendations for the improvement of data quality
- ensuring compliance with the `Data Protection Act 1998`, `Caldicott`, and other relevant security and confidentiality procedures

This maps directly to the candidate-profile requirement that the analyst should be able to sound like someone who:
- treats data quality and governance as part of the research-analytics job
- can identify and resolve issues affecting reporting consistency
- can support audit and controlled release over research-performance data
- can improve trust in the research-performance lane rather than only produce outputs from it

This note exists because that requirement is easy to flatten into the vague statement:
`good with data quality and governance`

That is not the real burden.

The employer is asking for something sharper:
- research-data issue identification
- audit and consistency checking
- governance-safe release control
- issue resolution with relevant staff
- bounded data-quality improvement support
