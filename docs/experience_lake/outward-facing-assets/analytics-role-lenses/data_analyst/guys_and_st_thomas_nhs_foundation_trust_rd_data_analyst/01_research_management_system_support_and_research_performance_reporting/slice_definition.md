## 1. Requirement Being Targeted

The requirement being targeted here is the Guy's and St Thomas' `R&D Data Analyst` expectation around:
- supporting and improving the `EDGE` research-management-system environment
- using and maintaining databases that back research-metric reporting and presentation
- supporting the reporting and presentation of `R&D` performance metrics
- submitting mandatory research-performance metrics to national bodies
- producing quarterly, monthly, annual, and ad hoc `R&D` performance reporting
- using a dashboard-backed reporting structure for `R&D` visibility

This maps directly to the candidate-profile requirement that the analyst should be able to sound like someone who:
- can run and improve a research-performance data environment rather than only build isolated reports
- can combine specialist system support with recurring high-accountability reporting delivery
- can keep research metrics usable for internal programme management and external accountability
- can work inside a controlled `R&D` reporting cycle rather than a loose BI backlog

This note exists because that requirement is easy to flatten into the vague statement:
`good at research reporting`

That is not the real burden.

The employer is asking for something sharper:
- research-management-system support
- system-backed research-performance reporting
- mandatory-return discipline
- dashboard-backed `R&D` visibility

The current platform does not contain a literal `EDGE`, `CPMS`, `NIHR`, `Department of Health`, `MHRA`, or live Guy's and St Thomas' `R&D` reporting estate, so the slice has to use an honest analogue.

- one governed specialist-system-support-style reporting lane
- one prepared research-performance-style reporting base built from multiple compact sources
- one mandatory-return-style or contractual-reporting-style summary surface
- one dashboard-style or management-reporting surface from the same controlled base
- one bounded reporting-cycle note that reads like `R&D` programme management support rather than generic BI output

So for this slice:
- `be responsible for implementation/support of EDGE` maps to `shape one bounded specialist-system-backed analytical product and support surface`
- `use and maintain databases to support research-metric reporting` maps to `materialise one prepared reporting base and one reusable reporting product from a governed data lane`
- `submit mandatory performance metrics to national bodies` maps to `materialise one mandatory-return-style summary from the same controlled base`
- `use the R&D Performance dashboard template` maps to `materialise one dashboard-style or management-information surface from that same base`

The claim must therefore remain:
- research-management-system shaped
- research-performance reporting shaped
- mandatory-return shaped
- careful not to imply literal system administration, national submission authority, or ownership of the whole Trust `R&D` data environment where this slice only proves one bounded system-backed reporting pack

Default first-pass posture:
- prepared research-performance base first
- recurring reporting product second
- mandatory-return-style surface third
- dashboard-style management reading fourth
- reporting-safe `R&D` cycle note fifth
- stronger live-system language only if the bounded evidence genuinely earns it
