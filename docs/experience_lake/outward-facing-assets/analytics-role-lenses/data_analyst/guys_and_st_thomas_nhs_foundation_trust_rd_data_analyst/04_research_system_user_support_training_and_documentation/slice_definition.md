## 1. Requirement Being Targeted

The requirement being targeted here is the Guy's and St Thomas' `R&D Data Analyst` expectation around:
- acting as a frontline advisory service for users of the research management system
- communicating professionally with all users of the research management system
- being a source of support for research staff when dealing with issues around data
- being responsible for Trust-wide training for users of the research management system
- being responsible for providing training to the `R&D Governance Team` for extensive use of the research management system
- reviewing, maintaining, and keeping up to date training material
- reviewing, maintaining, and keeping up to date procedural documentation
- assisting staff to collect and understand data consistency and analysis, providing training where necessary

This maps directly to the candidate-profile requirement that the analyst should be able to sound like someone who:
- can act as a local expert and frontline contact for users of a specialist research-management system
- can train users rather than only use the system personally
- can keep training and procedural materials current
- can help staff understand data consistency, quality, and analysis in practice

This note exists because that requirement is easy to flatten into the vague statement:
`good at stakeholder support`

That is not the real burden.

The employer is asking for something sharper:
- frontline advisory support
- user enablement and training
- procedural and training-document maintenance
- practical explanation of data consistency and analytical use
