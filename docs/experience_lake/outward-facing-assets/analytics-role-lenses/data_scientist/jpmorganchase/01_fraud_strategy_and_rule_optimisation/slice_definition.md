## 1. Requirement Being Targeted

The requirement being targeted here is the `JPMorganChase` `Data Scientist - Fraud Strategic Analytics Associate` expectation around:
- developing fraud strategies and rules
- implementing fraud strategies and rules
- ensuring fraud strategies and rules effectively detect fraudulent activities
- optimising transaction monitoring and controls
- reducing fraud and financial-crime risk across products and channels
- supporting the fraud strategy and control framework while preserving customer experience

This maps directly to the employer evidence that the candidate should be able to sound like someone who:
- can do more than describe fraud after the fact
- can design or improve working fraud decision logic
- can tune controls across products and channels
- can balance fraud detection effectiveness with customer experience
- can operate inside a first-line fraud-control setting rather than outside it

This note exists because that requirement is easy to flatten into the vague statement:
`good at fraud analytics and data science`

That is not the real burden.

The employer is asking for something sharper:
- fraud-strategy development
- rules and control optimisation
- detection-effectiveness improvement
- customer-experience-aware control tuning
- first-line strategy contribution inside a governed bank setting

The closest truthful analogue is:

- one control-reading note that explains why the chosen strategy posture is preferable

- `develop and implement fraud strategies and rules` maps to `one bounded strategy-and-rule-optimisation pack with explicit rule logic or strategy bands`

- `optimise transaction monitoring and controls` maps to `one threshold or ruleset comparison that changes which items are selected or escalated`
- `reduce fraud risk while preserving customer experience` maps to `one explicit trade-off reading between capture and burden`
- `support the fraud strategy and control framework` maps to `one controlled, documented optimisation pack rather than a one-off notebook`

The claim must therefore remain:
- fraud-strategy shaped
- rule-optimisation shaped
- effectiveness shaped
- trade-off shaped
- control-framework shaped
- careful not to imply a live banking decision engine, full product-strategy estate, or full first-line operating ownership where this slice only proves one bounded fraud-strategy optimisation object
