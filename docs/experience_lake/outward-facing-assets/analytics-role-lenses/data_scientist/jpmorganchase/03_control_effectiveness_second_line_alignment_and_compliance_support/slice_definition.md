- ensuring models are effective
- ensuring rulesets are effective
- ensuring strategies are effective
- supporting confirmation that compliance, audit, and control frameworks are followed
- using data to confirm adherence to regulatory standards

This maps directly to the employer evidence that the candidate should be able to sound like someone who:
- does more than tune rules
- can show that a chosen control posture remains effective under challenge
- can present first-line evidence in a way that second-line teams can test and accept

`good at fraud governance`
