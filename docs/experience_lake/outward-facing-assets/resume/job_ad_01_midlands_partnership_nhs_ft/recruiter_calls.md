## 1. Detailed Call List

1. Developed and deployed predictive and statistical models in real delivery settings using Python, R, SQL, and cloud tooling.
2. Built and maintained risk stratification, segmentation, and forecasting models that informed service planning, decision-making, or population-level action.
3. Designed or maintained analytical datasets, pipelines, transformations, and reproducible workflows that made analysis production-usable and auditable.
4. Extracted, cleaned, transformed, and linked large, complex datasets from multiple clinical, operational, corporate, or cross-system sources.
5. Used linked or population-level data not only to describe activity, but to identify unmet need, intervention opportunities, pathway patterns, or service-planning priorities.
6. Worked with NHS datasets, NHS data flows, NHS Data Dictionary definitions, and broader NHS data standards in a standards-aware way.
7. Worked within interoperability and cross-system data requirements rather than only single-system reporting environments.
8. Treated data quality as part of the analytical job by embedding validation, reconciliation, monitoring, anomaly detection, and fitness-for-use controls into analytical workflows.
9. Ensured models, feature sets, datasets, and transformations were reproducible, version-controlled, documented, and aligned to engineering standards such as Git or Azure DevOps.
10. Delivered governed and explainable AI or machine learning in settings where ethics, information governance, and model-risk expectations matter.
11. Operationalised advanced analytics into dashboards or decision-support products, including making outputs understandable to non-technical users.
12. Translated complex analytical findings into actionable recommendations for clinicians, managers, senior leaders, or wider operational stakeholders.
13. Collaborated with multidisciplinary teams including clinicians, managers, engineers, finance/performance teams, and external system partners to co-design useful analytics.
14. Led technically by mentoring, supervising, guiding quality, or developing less experienced analytical colleagues.
15. Worked comfortably in cloud and platform-shaped analytics environments using tools such as Azure ML, Databricks, Data Factory, Fabric, APIs, or similar engineering-adjacent tooling.
16. Exercised judgement as a specialist: choosing modelling approaches, handling ambiguous data, and balancing innovation with governance in high-accountability settings.
