Fresh start for **BNP Paribas Personal Finance - Data Scientist**.

This role is centred on **commercial customer analytics**, not finance data flows like Arval. The Data Scientist sits in the Analytics team and is expected to use advanced analytical methods to deliver commercial value, understand/test/predict/explain customer behaviours, build predictive models and business tools, maintain BAU model processes, use A/B testing where possible, and communicate clearly to senior leaders.

# 1. Business / problem screen

| BNP Personal Finance business problem | What the CV needs to prove |
| --- | --- |
| Understanding customer behaviour | You can analyse customer behaviour, customer knowledge, customer insight and behavioural levers |
| Delivering commercial value | Your models/analysis support business decisions, customer finance outcomes, conversion, risk, retention, targeting, or product performance |
| Consumer finance decisioning | You can work with customer/transaction/finance-style data in a way that supports responsible purchase finance decisions |
| Testing what works | You can run or approximate A/B testing, champion/challenger tests, holdout tests, backtests, or intervention comparisons |
| Predicting outcomes | You can build predictive models that estimate churn, propensity, risk, response, behaviour, conversion, or customer value |
| Explaining behaviour drivers | You can move beyond prediction into explaining the levers behind customer behaviour |
| Turning insight into business tools | You can convert models/analysis into dashboards, scoring workflows, decision tools, or stakeholder-ready outputs |
| Senior-leader communication | You can present insight clearly to senior leaders and non-technical business users |

The strongest business-positioning phrase is probably:

```text
customer behaviour, predictive modelling and commercial insight for consumer finance decisions
```

Not:

```text
fraud detection
payments and chargebacks
finance data flows
revenue protection
```

Those were right for other jobs, but not the centre here.
