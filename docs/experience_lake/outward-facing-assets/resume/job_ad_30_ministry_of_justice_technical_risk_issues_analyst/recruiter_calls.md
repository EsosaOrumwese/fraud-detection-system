# Recruiter Calls - Job Ad 30 - Ministry of Justice

Role:
- `Technical Risk & Issues Analyst`
- `Ministry of Justice`
- `HMCTS Digital and Technology Services`

Using the updated job-ad evidence doc plus the ideal candidate profile and Civil Service Success Profiles capture as the source of truth, the recruiter's calls for this role are:

1. Maintained disciplined risk, issue, action, or `RAID` registers, ensuring risks and issues were logged, categorised, prioritised, updated, tracked, and escalated reliably.
2. Supported practical risk and issue management processes rather than treating risk work as passive administration or a static spreadsheet exercise.
3. Assessed technical, operational, service, or digital risks by considering likelihood, impact, controls, ownership, mitigation, dependencies, and unresolved issues.
4. Used data, metrics, key indicators, dashboards, or `KPI`s to make risks and issues visible and support decision making.
5. Identified risk trends, recurring issues, interdependencies, or potential future risk scenarios through structured analysis rather than opinion alone.
6. Conducted investigation or cause-and-effect analysis to understand why risks, incidents, control gaps, or service issues occurred.
7. Tracked remediation activity, action owners, target dates, progress, exceptions, and whether improvements delivered the intended benefits.
8. Gathered, checked, organised, or documented evidence for audit, assurance, compliance, governance, or review activity.
9. Validated or reviewed controls, documentation, data integrity, access, process adherence, or other governance safeguards with strong attention to detail.
10. Worked in or near a technical, digital, operational, service-management, platform, or complex organisational environment where risks affected live services or business outcomes.
11. Understood or applied service-management discipline, including `ITIL`-style thinking, service levels, incident/problem/change context, or structured operational governance.
12. Communicated technical or operational risk information clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences.
13. Produced clear written risk summaries, issue updates, evidence notes, dashboard commentary, or senior-facing reporting outputs.
14. Facilitated or supported structured risk-review conversations, helping teams clarify risk rationale, control status, mitigation options, and next actions.
15. Worked collaboratively with product, service, operational, technical, governance, or assurance stakeholders to build a shared and current view of risks and issues.
16. Influenced others constructively by explaining why consistent risk capture, control evidence, timely updates, and escalation discipline mattered.
17. Contributed to process improvement, standardisation, guidance, documentation, or awareness activity that strengthened risk-management practice.
18. Showed awareness of emerging technology and `AI` risk, including ethical, security, operational, data-integrity, and control considerations.
19. Operated at `HEO` level by showing ownership, judgement, quality-service discipline, collaboration, and clear communication under ambiguity.
20. Demonstrated Civil Service behaviour evidence for `Managing a Quality Service`, `Working Together`, and `Communicating and Influencing` through specific examples and outcomes.

The spine of the call here is:

- technical risk and issue management in a government digital environment
- `RAID` register discipline, risk categorisation, prioritisation, action tracking, and escalation
- data-led risk reporting using metrics, indicators, dashboards, and `KPI`s
- cause-and-effect analysis, trend identification, and risk-scenario awareness
- audit, assurance, control validation, compliance evidence, and remediation tracking
- technical/service-management credibility, including `ITIL`-style operating discipline
- clear risk communication to technical and non-technical stakeholders
- structured collaboration with service owners, IT teams, governance bodies, and senior stakeholders
- emerging technology and `AI` risk awareness
- `HEO` Success Profiles behaviours: `Managing a Quality Service`, `Working Together`, and `Communicating and Influencing`
