# East Sussex Healthcare NHS Trust - Information Analyst Workbench

## Entry 1 - Role Surface and Scoring Priorities

This is a Band 5 Information Analyst role in Information Management & Performance at East Sussex Healthcare NHS Trust. It is less senior than the recent G7/Band 6 roles, but it is still technically demanding: the centre is advanced SQL, Excel, relational databases, accurate routine reporting, self-service dashboards, data quality, and supporting service improvement.

### Employer Problem

East Sussex needs an analyst who can help the Information Management & Performance team produce timely, accurate, actionable intelligence for managers and clinicians. The job is about preparing and analysing data from multiple sources, maintaining dashboards and interactive self-service reporting, supporting service improvement, and improving departmental data processing and reporting systems.

The real problem is:

> Can this person turn complex healthcare and operational data into accurate reports, dashboards and analysis that help clinical, financial and performance teams make better decisions, while maintaining data quality, confidentiality and reporting standards?

### Role Identity

This is not a leadership-heavy role. It is a hands-on analyst role with strong technical and reporting expectations.

The strongest identity for Eric would be:

> A technically strong information analyst with advanced SQL, Excel, relational database, dashboard, data-quality and problem-solving experience, able to work independently, handle large datasets, support managers and clinicians, and contribute to better self-service reporting and predictive modelling.

East Sussex Healthcare needs someone in Information Management & Performance who can support senior analysts by preparing routine and ad-hoc reports, maintaining dashboards and self-service interactive reporting, improving data processing/reporting systems, supporting service improvement, and ensuring managers and clinicians receive actionable intelligence.

## Entry 3 - Supporting Statement Structure

For this East Sussex Band 5 Information Analyst role, the statement should be hands-on, technical, accurate and service-focused. The advert is buying advanced SQL, Excel, relational databases, large dataset analysis, dashboards, data quality, independent working, deadlines and communication with managers/clinicians. NHS experience is desirable, not essential, so the statement should not over-apologise for it.

### Main Argument

> I am a strong Information Analyst candidate because I can prepare, validate, analyse and present complex data from multiple sources using SQL, Microsoft SQL Server, Excel, relational databases and dashboards, while maintaining data quality, confidentiality and clear communication with service users.

### Suggested Structure

| Paragraph | Argument | Evidence to use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Opening fit | I understand this role is about accurate, timely, actionable intelligence for managers and clinicians, plus dashboards and self-service reporting. | South Western, SQL, Excel, Power BI, data quality. |
| 2. Education and technical foundation | I meet the Maths, English and degree-level requirements. | WASSCE Maths A, WASSCE English A, IGCSE Maths A, BEng, MSc Data Science. |
| 3. Core analyst experience | I have substantial experience analysing large datasets to support decisions. | South Western operational reporting, public-data project, platform. |
| 4. SQL, Microsoft SQL Server, Excel and relational databases | I can use the core tools this role says are pivotal. | SQL, MSSQL, Excel formulas, pivot tables, lookups, Access, relational database work. |
| 5. Data quality and validation | I can check whether reports are accurate before users rely on them. | Row counts, duplicates, missing fields, failed joins, true zero versus missing submission, source reconciliation. |
| 6. Dashboards, self-service reporting and improvement | I can help improve reporting systems and make information usable. | Power BI dashboards, platform dashboards, self-service views, drill-downs, data-quality measures. |
| 7. NHS awareness, confidentiality and Data Protection | I understand NHS information work requires patient-data caution, data standards and confidentiality. | Data Protection Act 2018 understanding, GDPR, access control, NHS data standards preparation. |
| 8. Communication, prioritisation and support | I can work independently, meet deadlines, explain findings and support users or junior colleagues. | South Western stakeholder discussions, trainee support, Five Guys pressure/training. |
| 9. Values close | I would work in line with East Sussex values. | Working Together, Respect and Compassion, Engagement and Involvement, Improvement and Development. |

| Evidence source | Weight |

This should be more direct than the G7 statement. The centre of gravity is not analytical leadership; it is accurate hands-on information analysis for clinical, financial and performance users.
