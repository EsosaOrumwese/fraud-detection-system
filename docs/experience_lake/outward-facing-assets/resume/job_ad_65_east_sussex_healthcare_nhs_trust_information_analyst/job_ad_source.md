## Job Summary

An exciting opportunity has arisen for an experienced analyst to work within the Information Management & Performance Team at East Sussex Healthcare NHS Trust. This is an innovative role where creativity and initiative will be encouraged to help shape future self-service interactive reporting and predictive modelling needs.

## Main Duties of the Job

The Trust is looking for a bright team player who is enthusiastic, analytical, has strong numerical ability, and can thrive in a busy, fast-paced environment.

An NHS Trust background would be an advantage, but is not essential.

The role requires advanced SQL and Excel skills, as these are pivotal. The postholder must be able to prioritise, work to tight deadlines, demonstrate excellent organisational skills, and maintain high attention to detail.

## About the Trust

Staff benefits include staff development, enrolment in the NHS pension scheme, auto-enrolment to the Temporary Workforce Service, onsite nurseries, staff restaurants and accommodation.

Staff also have access to an internal Occupational Health department and free psychological support through a confidential Employee Assistance Programme. Onsite parking is available from £50 a year.

## Person Specification

### Qualifications - Essential

- Good general level of education including maths and English.
- Evidence of continued professional development to degree level or equivalent level of knowledge and experience.

### Experience - Essential

- 3-5 years' experience of undertaking analysis of data to support business decision making using large data sets.
- Experienced in the use of relational databases and MS Excel spreadsheet.
- Knowledge / experience of Microsoft SQL Server.

### Experience - Desirable

- 3-5 years' experience of working in the NHS.
- Knowledge / experience of NHS datasets and care pathways.
- Experience of the development and release cycle.
- Experience of Microsoft SQL reporting platforms.

### Skills - Essential

- Ability to exercise own initiative and work independently to achieve objectives and deadlines.
- Ability to manage both a high-quality service to meet the demands of the business division and maintain a close professional working relationship with the wider information service.
- Skills in developing, maintaining and manipulating highly accurate and complex information systems such as databases or spreadsheets.
- Good working knowledge and skills in MSSQL and MS Excel.
- Excellent problem-solving skills for the development of advanced analytical methodologies.
- Excellent communication skills.
- High level of interpersonal skills.
- Ability to plan, prioritise and assign tasks to support staff.
- Ability to work and concentrate in a busy and demanding environment.

### Knowledge - Essential

- General understanding of the NHS and key developments shaping the information agenda.
- NHS information and data modelling concepts.
- NHS data standards and terminology, including clinical datasets.
- Thorough understanding and knowledge of relational databases, information reporting and advanced queries.
- Good knowledge of the Data Protection Act 2018 and ability to work confidentially with patient-level information.

### Knowledge - Desirable

- NHS Data Dictionary.
- Working knowledge of R language and concepts.
- Working knowledge of ASP.net.
