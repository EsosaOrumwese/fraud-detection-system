# LexisNexis Risk Solutions Data Scientist - 3 Screens

This is the job-ad interpretation stage only: business/risk problem screen, technical/model capability screen, and stakeholder/operating-style screen. No proof buckets, evidence ledger, scoring, or bullets yet.

## 1. Business / Risk Problem Screen

This is a **fraud/risk Data Scientist** role, not a general Data Scientist role.

LexisNexis Risk Solutions works across AML/CTF, identity authentication and verification, fraud, credit risk mitigation and customer data management. The team uses global data from a large real-time fraud detection platform, and the role is expected to create real-world impact through **lower customer friction, reduced fraud losses and increased customer profitability**. The platform analyses billions of transactions per month across financial services, insurance, e-commerce and on-demand services.

So the CV eventually needs to prove evidence around:

| LexisNexis business/risk problem | What the CV needs to show later |
| --- | --- |
| Fraud loss reduction | You can identify, rank, score or flag risky behaviours, transactions, accounts or cases. |
| Customer friction reduction | You understand the trade-off between catching fraud and not blocking good customers. |
| Customer profitability | You can connect fraud/risk decisions to value, cost exposure, false positives or review burden. |
| Real-time fraud detection | You have evidence of scoring, monitoring, streaming/near-real-time decisioning or production-shaped fraud workflows. |
| Enterprise customer risk problems | You can package analysis into validation reports, risk summaries, presentations or model rationale. |
| Evolving fraudster behaviour | You can define behavioural features that capture account, transaction, usage or anomaly patterns. |
| Financial services / e-commerce / payments risk | You can speak through concrete fraud, transaction, payment, chargeback, account or behaviour evidence. |

The strongest business-positioning phrase is likely:

```text
fraud-risk data science for real-time decisioning, behavioural features, model/rule evaluation and customer-friction trade-offs
```

Not:

```text
generic machine learning
business intelligence reporting
customer analytics
commercial KPI dashboards
```
