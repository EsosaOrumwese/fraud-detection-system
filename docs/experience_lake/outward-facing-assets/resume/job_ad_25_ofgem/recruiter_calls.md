# Recruiter Calls - Job Ad 25 - OFGEM

Role:
- `Analyst`
- `OFGEM`

Using the updated job-ad evidence doc plus the ideal candidate profile as the source of truth, the recruiter’s calls for this role are:

1. Worked with large and complex datasets to monitor supplier, consumer, and market performance in a regulated environment.
2. Contributed to regular BAU monitoring and reporting activity rather than only producing one-off analysis.
3. Monitored competition indicators, customer-protection indicators, and market-performance signals to identify issues, trends, or emerging concerns.
4. Worked with reporting frameworks used to collect and analyse data from regulated organisations or licensees.
5. Issued or supported information requests to third parties and used the resulting evidence to strengthen monitoring, reporting, or decision-making.
6. Produced intelligence updates, analytical reports, senior briefings, and regulatory decision-support outputs.
7. Translated analysis into evidence that informed policy, regulation, pricing, consumer protection, or senior decision-making.
8. Supported assessment of the impacts of retail energy policies and regulation.
9. Handled unplanned analytical requests at pace while still producing clear, proportionate, and actionable outputs.
10. Used analytical judgement under uncertainty to solve problems, formulate pragmatic options, and present actionable information to decision makers.
11. Scoped analytical input with internal stakeholders and agreed the evidence needed for monitoring, policy, or regulatory purposes.
12. Built effective relationships with internal and external stakeholders, including policy, compliance, suppliers, consumer bodies, and government stakeholders.
13. Communicated analytical insight clearly to non-expert audiences through written and verbal outputs.
14. Used practical analytical tooling such as Excel, Power BI, and Python or R to manage, manipulate, analyse, and present data.
15. Improved or sought to improve the efficiency and accuracy of data collection and analysis processes.
16. Brought public-interest judgement to analysis, connecting market evidence to outcomes for consumers, communities, fairness, and society.

The spine of the call here is:

- regulatory analytics and market intelligence
- large-dataset monitoring of supplier, consumer, and market performance
- competition and consumer-protection reporting
- policy, pricing, and regulatory decision support
- rapid-response analysis under uncertainty
- pragmatic options and actionable recommendations
- stakeholder engagement across Ofgem, government, suppliers, and consumer bodies
- Excel / Power BI / Python or R for practical analysis
- public-interest framing around consumers, fairness, and market outcomes
