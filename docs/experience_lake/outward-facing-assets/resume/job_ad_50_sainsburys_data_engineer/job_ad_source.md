## Role Snapshot

Sainsbury's frames this as a Data Engineer role inside its data engineering teams, working in a modern engineering environment serving a multi-channel, multi-brand retailer with large-scale customer, sales and loyalty data. The ad emphasises the scale of Sainsbury's data estate, including over 1.2 billion transactions each year.

The role contributes to technology that improves customer experience, efficiency and cost reduction. It is especially focused on real-time analytics for sales data, data pipelines, stream processing and Snowflake-based warehousing.
