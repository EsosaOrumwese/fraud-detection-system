# Sandwell and West Birmingham NHS Trust - Senior Information Analyst Workbench

## Entry 1 - Role Surface and Scoring Priorities

I have read the advert, person specification and JD. This one is **very scoreable**, but it is also **more specific than the previous Hywel Dda role**. It is not just "senior reporting analyst." It is a **Senior Information Analyst for Surgical Services and Women and Child Health**, with heavy emphasis on **contracting, maternity data submissions, waiting list processes, national guidance, SSRS, SSIS, SQL, Power BI, Excel, data quality, supervision and statutory reporting continuity**.

## Employer Problem

The Trust needs someone who can protect and improve the information function for high-stakes operational reporting. The problem is:

> Can this person produce accurate, validated, nationally compliant reports for Surgical Services and Women and Child Health, support contracting and maternity submissions, explain performance trends, investigate anomalies, and keep statutory reporting running even when senior staff are absent?

This is a **delivery and assurance role**. They need someone who can extract, validate, interpret, automate, publish, explain and defend figures. The advert names contracting activity submissions, maternity services dataset submissions, performance monitoring returns, assurance commentary for boards/regulators, and urgent executive or regulatory requests.

## Role Identity

The centre of gravity is:

> **NHS performance and information analyst with senior reporting responsibility, technical reporting tools, data-quality control, national guidance interpretation, and junior staff supervision.**

The JD page 1 also says the post supervises junior staff, manages workload and training needs, deputises for department managers, supports clinical groups, and develops monitoring reports and data collection linked to national targets.

So this is not mainly a data science role. It is:

- performance reporting;
- data extraction and validation;
- statutory and national returns;
- waiting list or contracting processes;
- maternity services dataset submissions;
- report automation and publication;
- junior staff supervision;
- assurance commentary;
- clinical and senior stakeholder communication.
