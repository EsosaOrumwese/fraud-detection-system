## Job Details

**Job Title:** Senior Information Analyst

**Base:** Sandwell Hospital

**Division:** Information Department IM&T

**Contracted Hours:** 37.5 Hours

**Grade:** Band 6

**Responsible to:** Information and Data Quality Manager

**Accountable to:** Head of Information

## Job Summary

To work with the Head of Information and the Information Services Manager to develop a service which actively supports the Trusts Corporate Business Plan whilst maintaining the provision of the departments standard monitoring and reporting functions.

To supervise junior staff, managing their workload and training needs. To deputise for the department managers as required.

The post holder will provide information management support for the Clinical Groups. This will involve building good working relationships with Group staff, advising them on their information needs and ensuring these are met.

The post holder will be actively involved in meeting the information reporting requirements associated with national targets. This includes the planning and development of data collection and performance monitoring reports together with expansion of the trust's intranet.

## Main Responsibilities

### 1. Information Analysis for Ad Hoc Requests

The post holder will contribute to this core function of the Information Department. Emphasis being placed on delegating work to junior members of the team, prioritising ad hoc work and training other members of the team to undertake more complex analysis. When deadlines are compromised the post holder will undertake ad hoc requests as required.

1.1. Receive ad hoc requests for information by telephone, email or face to face and accurately log requirements on the Information Department request line.

1.2. Liaise with a diverse range of staff including managers and clinicians, to interpret highly complex requirements, advise of information available and negotiate achievable deadlines. Use own judgment to disseminate sensitive information within confidentiality guidance.

1.3. Use specialist knowledge of the extensive range of data collected and processed to identify a suitable information source, taking into account site specific requirements and historical changes to data collection.

Analyse data using a wide range of database tools and analysis techniques. Create complex queries and reports using SQL, SQL Server Reporting Services (SSRS) and MS Excel.

1.4. Present accurate information in the most appropriate format for the request. Preparing graphs to demonstrate trends, calculating percentages and variances, projecting out-turn and providing explanatory notes regarding data ambiguities.

1.5. Confirm data quality by checking against comparable data or referring to another member of the information team to check analysis work.

1.6. File data according to the Information Department policy to ensure further work can be undertaken.

1.7. Ensure that requests assigned to themselves are appropriately progress chased.

1.8. Undertake requests requiring highly complex data manipulation and interpretation. Seeking appropriate support from the programming team for requests requiring programming input.

1.9. Advise, manage and prioritise requests referred up by Information Officers and Information Analysts.

### 2. Information Analysis Support to Clinical Groups

2.1. To build good working relationships with Group Directors of Operations and their support teams, through appropriate and agreed active involvement in initiatives requiring information management input.

2.2. Develop a wide ranging knowledge of information available from PAS systems, SQL databases and other department systems relevant to the specific group.

2.3. Interpret complex or ambiguous requirements and apply specialist knowledge to advise the Group on their information needs, particularly in relation to appropriate data sources, methods of analysis, data quality, presentation and interpretation of information, including Clinical or other Indicators.

2.4. Prepare highly complex analysis of data from various sources, working accurately to present information in the most appropriate format for the Group to understand and discuss. Generally presenting data in report or graphical form, with appropriate explanatory notes and interpretation.

2.5. Attend meetings with large groups to discuss information requirements and present information analysed, sometimes outside own area of specialist knowledge.

2.6. Actively contribute information support for service developments taking place within the Group.

2.7. Ensure the regular routine and ad-hoc workload from the Group is managed appropriately within the ad hoc requests process, delegating work to Information Officers and providing expert knowledge / training where required.

2.8. Undertake and prioritising requests from the division when deadlines are compromised.

### 3. Supervise and Support the Information Team

3.1. Supervise the ad hoc work of the team. Providing guidance and support to the Information Officer responsible for prioritising ad hoc requests, to ensure the efficient running of the departments procedure for logging and processing such requests.

3.2. Take an active role in development, testing and implementation of core reporting procedures including; production of commissioning and waiting list minimum data sets and other in-house systems for processing data.

3.3. Understand commissioning guidance, including providing information support for financial planning, casemix analysis and using nationally issued software. Organise development of commissioning program and processes to incorporate changes at the start of each financial year, delegating appropriate tasks to Information Officers.

3.4. Supporting role in managing change as a result of organisation restructuring, rationalising process and procedures.

3.5. Support the development of Information Officers / Analysts by providing training to ensure they can carry out their roles.

3.6. Support other members of team by providing expert advice in areas of specialist knowledge and checking work.

3.7. Update the electronic knowledge base with details of how to do ad hoc requests and example queries and explanatory notes. Ensure existing requests in the knowledge base are kept up to date.

3.8. Keep informed of mandated changes to national returns and communicate these to Information and Trust staff. Ensure local and national changes to returns or reports are implemented by the team.

3.9. Raise awareness of department procedures, ensure these are adhered to and are updated as appropriate. Ensure staff members follow Trust policies and procedures.

3.10. Assist with the recruitment process, sit on interview panels and organise initial induction of new recruits.

3.11. Deputise for the Information Manager, in whose absence the post holder will manage the information team to ensure critical deadlines are achieved.

### 4. Development of Monitoring Systems for National Targets

4.1. Develop information required to monitor and achieve national targets.

4.2. Interpret guidance and attend meetings to discuss requirements and progress.

4.3. Support the provisional data collection facilities required by these national targets.

4.4. Develop reports to monitor these targets, as well as reports on the quality of the data collected.

4.5. Reinforce to members of staff the importance of collecting additional or amended information and the advantages of increased data quality.

### 5. Manage Projects

5.1. Be the lead contact for a number of projects, both long and short-term, developing data collection systems, data extraction processes and reports to meet the specific need of the project.

5.2. Plan and organise the introduction of new information systems both within the information department and more widely across the Trust. Devise and develop appropriate reporting.

### 6. Management Report Development

6.1. Be the named contact for key managers within the Trust to ensure management reporting requirements are understood, planned, developed and incorporated into the routine running of the Department.

6.2. Take a pro-active role in the development and improvement of reporting Information on the Trust Intranet/Extranet including scheduling SSRS reports. Promote the use of the Intranet for disseminating information.

6.3. Play an active role in the implementation and further development of any new analytical or reporting tools.

6.4. Make a wider range of information available to staff to meet the expanding requirements of the Trust.

6.5. Participate in the ongoing development of the Trust's PAS and other information systems. Managing data flows and accuracy during implementation and providing appropriate training to clinical and non clinical staff.

### 7. Produce External Statutory Returns

7.1. Complete annual and quarterly external statutory returns, delegating where appropriate.

7.2. Check statutory returns prepared by junior members of the team, ensure deadlines are met and changes in guidance are implemented.

7.3. Identify changes in data that may affect returns. Resolve issues identified and prepare appropriate comments under the guidance of the Information Manager.

### 8. General Duties

8.1. To put high emphasis on data quality and accuracy, through monitoring and checking of information collected on the PAS and other systems. Undertake data audits as required.

8.2. Assist with the documentation of guidelines and procedures in support of Information processes including the improvement of Data Quality.

8.3. Provide a comparative analysis information service to the Trust utilising both the regional databases and other Information sources, e.g., CHKS.

8.4. Oversee data collection and submission for winter pressures.

8.5. Organise and provide cover for core functions of the Department when other members of the team are absent.

8.6. Undertake any other duties as appropriate to the broad objectives of the post.

### 9. Personal Development and General

9.1. Agree Personal Development Objectives with the Deputy Information Manager in yearly appraisal and six monthly reviews.

9.2. Ensure that Personal skills training and IT Training, identified in the Development Review, are attended and used appropriately.

9.3. The post holder must conform to Trust Policies and Procedures and abide by the Caldicott Principles and the Information Security procedures.

## Working Relationships - Internal/External

The post holder will work closely with other members of the Information Team, and will supply information to Trust staff at all levels, Health Authorities and GPs.
