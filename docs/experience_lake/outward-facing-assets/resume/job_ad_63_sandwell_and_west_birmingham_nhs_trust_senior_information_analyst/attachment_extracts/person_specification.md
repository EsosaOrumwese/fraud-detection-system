## Post Details

**Post Title:** Senior Information Analyst

**Division/Department:** Information Department

## Attributes

### Experience

Specify the relevant experience required to be competent in role.

#### Essential

- Substantive relevant information analysis experience in an Acute Information Department. Evidence of detailed specialist knowledge of waiting list or contracting processes; including national guidance and reporting requirements.
- Advanced experience of a wide range of report writing and analysis tools including SQL, SQL Server Reporting Services (SSRS) and MS Excel.
- Competent with other PC software packages including text editors, MS Word, Internet and Email.
- ECDL or equivalent qualification.
- Proven ability and confidence to work with numbers, formulas, percentages, variances, trends and graphs. Experience of working with large reports of figures.
- Proven experience of validating figures on complex reports.
- Ability to assimilate and interpret highly complex or ambiguous information across a wide range of data.
- Data quality processes and audits.

**How identified:** AF, I

### Qualifications

Specify the qualifications required to be competent in role.

#### Essential

- Maths and English GCSE grade 'C' or above.
- Numerate A levels.
- Degree.
- Willing to complete supervisory management qualification.

### Personal Qualities

#### Essential

- Highly numerate, working to a high degree of accuracy.
- Self motivated.
- Capable of working in a structured manner.
- Capacity to work under pressure to tight deadlines.
- Use own initiative.
- Problem solving skills.
- Able to take a lead role within the team.
- Advanced interpersonal skills including the ability to negotiate.

### Management / Supervision / Coordination Skills

Specify the level/type of skills required.

#### Essential

- Plan and manage own workload effectively. Making decisions in own area and sometimes making decisions that impact on others.
- Project Management Skills.
- Able to motivate self and others.
- Able to train and support junior members of team.
- Oversee ad-hoc request work flow and resolve problems referred up by other members of the team.
- In the absence of more senior managers: co-ordinate department work flow to ensure national reporting requirements achieved.
- Assist on interview panels.

#### Desirable

- Prince2 Foundation.

### Written Skills

Specify the level of written skills, requiring the use of English, routinely required by the post-holder.

#### Essential

- Ability to explain complex analysis in a clear written manner.
- Proficient in documenting processes/tasks to aid delegation.
- Able to read and interpret national guidance.

### Communication / Verbal Skills

Specify the extent and frequency the post holder will be required to use a range of communication skills.

#### Essential

- Excellent oral and written communication skills.
- Able to communicate face to face, by telephone and e-mail in a concise and informative manner.
- Attend internal and external meetings: presenting highly complex information to large groups (10 or more).
- Able to communicate with a diverse range of staff to explain highly complex information to specialists and non specialists.
- Confident to communicate at a senior level.
- Ability to negotiate with senior managers, clinicians and other staff.
- Able to issue instructions to more junior members of the information team.
- Capacity to explain processes and concepts to Information staff and more widely across the Trust.

### Knowledge

Specify any specialist knowledge the post holder will be required to hold.

#### Essential

- Ability to grasp concepts quickly.
- Broad and detailed knowledge of NHS national patient based returns and NHS data definitions such as the Data Manual and NHS Information notices.
- Detailed knowledge of all PAS modules.
- Demonstrate expert knowledge in certain areas of Information work. Ability to advise other members of the department in these areas.
- Understanding of patient flows in an acute hospital environment.
