# Arriva Group — Fraud Analyst: 3 Screens

## 1. Business / problem screen

Arriva’s role is centred on **revenue protection through fraud prevention and detection across the CrossCountry rail network**. The ad says the analyst will provide insight, track emerging trends, protect revenue, identify fraudulent activity across the network, and evidence the impact of anti-fraud initiatives. 

So the CV must prove evidence around:

| Arriva business problem                   | What the CV needs to show                                                                                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Revenue loss from fraudulent activity     | You can use data to identify fraud risk and support loss mitigation                                               |
| Fraud across a physical/transport network | You can analyse behaviour by route, location, process, account, journey, or operational segment                   |
| Individual fraud cases                    | You can produce subject profiles, case evidence, or investigation packs                                           |
| Wider fraud trends and patterns           | You can detect recurring patterns, anomalies, emerging risks, or control weaknesses                               |
| Weaknesses in processes and controls      | You can quantify gaps in current checks, reports, processes, handovers, or monitoring                             |
| Anti-fraud initiative impact              | You can evidence recovered/generated value, reduced leakage, improved detection, or better intervention targeting |
| Senior-manager reporting                  | You can turn complex fraud/risk analysis into clear reporting for decision-makers                                 |

The strongest business positioning for Arriva is:

```text
fraud risk analytics, revenue protection, subject profiling and route/process risk detection
```

Not just:

```text
fraud detection modelling
```

because Arriva’s ad is more operational and revenue-protection focused than Trustpilot.

The first four should almost certainly appear in the CV because they match the core of the role: fraud risk, complex data, indicators/trends, and clear reporting.

Buckets 5–7 are the Arriva-specific differentiators. They are what would make the CV feel like it was written for **CrossCountry Revenue Protection**, not just for any fraud analyst role.
