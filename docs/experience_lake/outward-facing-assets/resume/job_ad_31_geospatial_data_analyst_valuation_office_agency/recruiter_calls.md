# Recruiter Calls - Job Ad 31 - Valuation Office Agency

Role:
- `Geospatial Data Analyst`
- `Valuation Office Agency`
- `HMRC Valuation Office`
- `Information, Data & Analysis`

Using the updated job-ad evidence doc plus the ideal candidate profile and Civil Service Success Profiles capture as the source of truth, the recruiter's calls for this role are:

1. **Used `Python` or `R` to undertake data analysis, with this programming-led analysis clearly visible as the lead sift requirement.**

2. **Applied programming to practical analytical problems rather than only listing `Python` or `R` as a tool.**

3. **Worked with geospatial data across the data lifecycle, including sourcing, preparing, processing, updating, refining, correcting, publishing, and maintaining data quality.**

4. **Used `GIS` software such as `ArcGIS`, `QGIS`, or comparable tools to analyse, prepare, visualise, or quality-check spatial data.**

5. **Treated geospatial data as a specialist data type, with awareness of spatial accuracy, geometry, topology, completeness, and map-based interpretation issues.**

6. **Transformed and integrated data from multiple sources, including combining spatial and non-spatial data into usable analytical outputs.**

7. **Built or used automated workflows and repeatable processes for data preparation, transformation, analysis, visualisation, or quality assurance.**

8. **Created bespoke visualisations, maps, or spatial outputs that made geospatial information usable for business or service decisions.**

9. **Defined and carried out quality-assurance activities, including verification, validation, analytical review, completeness checks, and error correction.**

10. **Checked or reasoned about topological integrity and geospatial data correctness rather than relying only on row-level data checks.**

11. **Implemented or followed analytical standards and data standards so outputs were consistent, reusable, and trustworthy.**

12. **Produced or contributed to metadata and technical documentation for datasets, processes, workflows, assumptions, limitations, and quality checks.**

13. **Organised analytical work to meet expected timelines while maintaining accuracy and attention to detail.**

14. **Used strong analytical and problem-solving skills to diagnose data-quality issues, integration problems, workflow failures, or inconsistencies in spatial outputs.**

15. **Communicated geospatial or technical findings clearly in writing and verbally to technical and non-technical audiences.**

16. **Worked with internal or external stakeholders to understand geospatial requirements, user needs, business priorities, or service-transformation goals.**

17. **Transferred knowledge effectively by explaining data, workflows, tools, quality checks, or outputs so others could reuse or trust the work.**

18. **Connected geospatial data work to public-sector property valuation, business transformation, service improvement, or operational decision support.**

19. **Demonstrated `HEO`-level `Making Effective Decisions` by using credible evidence, considering options and risks, and presenting timely recommendations.**

20. **Demonstrated `Managing a Quality Service` by maintaining reliable data processes, resolving risks or issues, meeting user needs, and improving service quality.**

21. **Demonstrated `Communicating and Influencing` by explaining technical geospatial work with clear purpose, appropriate detail, and checked understanding.**

The spine of the call here is:

- `Python` or `R` analysis as the lead sift criterion
- specialist geospatial data work, not generic analysis with a map attached
- `GIS` tooling such as `ArcGIS` or `QGIS`
- full geospatial data lifecycle: source, prepare, process, update, refine, correct, publish
- spatial and non-spatial data transformation and integration
- automated workflows and repeatable data processes
- geospatial QA, validation, completeness, standards, and topological integrity
- metadata, technical documentation, and knowledge transfer
- stakeholder requirement understanding and clear communication
- public-sector property valuation and business-transformation context
- `HEO` Success Profiles behaviours: `Making Effective Decisions`, `Managing a Quality Service`, and `Communicating and Influencing`
