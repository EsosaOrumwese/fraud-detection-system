## Job Description

The role is an integral part of the Women's and Children's team within the Business Intelligence unit at Cheltenham General Hospital.

The postholder is primarily responsible for providing information to the organisation and external stakeholders.

The role involves working closely with clinicians and senior managers within the hospital to understand requirements, develop reporting tools and provide intelligence that enables services to make better informed decisions.

The postholder must be engaging, forward-thinking and positive in order to build strong working relationships with customers and improve patient care.

The postholder will interrogate large volumes of highly complex data and use a variety of software and analytical methods to turn this data into intelligence.

The postholder is expected to highlight concerns about data quality and accuracy and take appropriate action as required.

The postholder will work as part of a team of analysts but must also work independently at times and manage their own workload.

The postholder must take a proactive approach to prioritising and negotiating deadlines.
