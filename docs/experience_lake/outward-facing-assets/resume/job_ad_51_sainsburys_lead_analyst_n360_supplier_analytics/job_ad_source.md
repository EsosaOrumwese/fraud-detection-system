## Role Snapshot

Sainsbury's frames this as a Lead Analyst role in Nectar360 Supplier Analytics, sitting inside a retail media and consultancy proposition for General Merchandise clients. Nectar360 is described as a loyalty, insights and media services business connected to Sainsbury's customer data and commercial decision-making.

The role combines hands-on analytics, client partnership, retail media measurement, customer insight propositions, analytical roadmaps, stakeholder management and continuous improvement.
