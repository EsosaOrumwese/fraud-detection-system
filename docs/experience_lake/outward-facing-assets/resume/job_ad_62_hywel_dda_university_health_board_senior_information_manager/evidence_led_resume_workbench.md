# Hywel Dda University Health Board - Senior Information Manager Workbench

## Entry 1 - Role Surface and Scoring Priorities

I have read the pasted advert and the attached JD/person specification. This role is **not mainly a technical data science role** and it is not the same as the BCUHB MHLD Specialist Information Analyst role. This is a **Health Board-wide Senior Information Manager role** in Digital Services / Information Services, focused on corporate reporting, reporting governance, national guidance, accurate outputs, staff support, and influencing the organisation to use information properly.

## Employer's Problem

Hywel Dda needs someone to help strengthen its **corporate information management framework** so reporting across the Health Board is accurate, timely, definition-compliant, and useful for accountability against internal and external targets. The role exists because information reporting needs to support business planning, operational management, performance management, service improvement, national requirements, and external reporting.

The employer's problem is not simply "we need an analyst." It is closer to:

> "We need a Band 6 information manager who can make corporate reporting reliable, interpret national reporting requirements, manage reporting outputs, influence clinical and non-clinical managers, support staff to use reports, supervise others, and ensure reporting reflects accurate data and agreed definitions."

That means this application should major on **information management, reporting assurance, Excel/database work, national guidance, stakeholder influence, training/mentoring, conflicting priorities, and data quality**.

## Role Identity

The job title is **Senior Information Manager**, Band 6. The organisation chart places the role under the Corporate Information Manager and shows responsibility for an Information Manager, Information Officers, Senior Information Analyst and Information Analyst. So this is more management/co-ordination-heavy than a normal analyst post.

The post holder supports and deputises for the Corporate Information Manager, delivers regular and ad-hoc reports to clinical, managerial and external audiences, supports the development of reporting systems, interprets national requirements, and helps staff across the organisation access and use information reports.

Hywel Dda's values in the JD are:

- **Dignity, Respect and Fairness**
- **Integrity, Openness and Honesty**
- **Caring, Kindness and Compassion**

The organisational values are:

For this role, values translate into practical behaviour:

- **Integrity and honesty:** do not release weak, misleading or unvalidated reports.
- **Openness:** make assumptions, limitations, data-quality issues and definition risks visible.
- **Respect and fairness:** communicate data issues without blaming teams.
- **Working together:** share best practice across Information Services and work with remote teams.
- **Putting people first:** remember reporting supports patients, clinicians, managers and service quality.

## What Not to Overemphasise

For this job, do **not** major on machine learning, AWS, fraud platform architecture, or advanced modelling unless those examples are translated into corporate reporting value.

The platform can help, but only for:

- reporting frameworks;
- dashboard design;
- validation checks;
- data-quality monitoring;
- access controls;
- documentation;
- translating complex information into action.

The panel is not primarily buying a data scientist. They are buying a **corporate information manager**.
