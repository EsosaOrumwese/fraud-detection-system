## Source-Weighted Reading Note

- shaping validated data into accessible dashboard-style and infographic-style products
- translating analysis into guided, non-technical explanation
- supporting demonstrations, walkthroughs, and practical user confidence
- improving usability through likely-user-friction prompts and support materials
