2. **Used `SQL` practically to query, extract, join, validate, and analyse structured datasets rather than only listing it as a technical skill.**

3. **Developed, maintained, improved, or redeveloped databases or database-like analytical systems used for recurring surveillance, reporting, or operational analysis.**

4. **Managed enhanced surveillance-style data flows, including ongoing datasets that require accuracy, completeness, updating, and repeatable management rather than one-off analysis.**

5. **Worked with complex scientific, public-health, pandemic, laboratory, surveillance, or health-adjacent data and interpreted it carefully for evidence-based reporting.**

6. **Analysed and interpreted complex data in a way that supported scientific reports, national or international meetings, ad hoc analysis, publications, projects, or grant/reporting outputs.**

7. **Produced regular and ad hoc reports from governed datasets, with clear explanation of what the data showed, what limitations remained, and what interpretation was justified.**

8. **Applied strong attention to detail and a particular emphasis on data accuracy, including validation, reconciliation, completeness checks, anomaly checks, and documented assumptions.**

9. **Handled sensitive or governed information with confidentiality, data security, and statutory/data-protection discipline, even where the candidate's direct evidence is adjacent rather than direct patient-data handling.**

10. **Worked in or could credibly adapt to a high-consequence scientific or public-health environment where data quality affects surveillance, outbreak understanding, and public-health decision support.**

11. **Supported surveillance, pandemic preparedness, outbreak-related analysis, or public-health interpretation through data work rather than treating the role as generic database administration.**

12. **Integrated multiple datasets from different sources, including public-health or pandemic-related sources, and made careful methodological choices about definitions, metrics, missingness, outliers, and comparability.**

13. **Demonstrated scientific grounding through a science/engineering/data-science education route and evidence of structured analytical reasoning.**

14. **Worked with time-series or longitudinal data, including recurring daily or periodic records where trend, coverage, and temporal consistency mattered.**

15. **Collaborated or communicated effectively with technical, scientific, operational, or non-technical audiences by turning complex data into clear written outputs, presentations, or decision-support explanations.**

16. **Contributed to project organisation, documentation, and reproducible analytical processes so data work could be reviewed, repeated, and trusted by others.**

17. **Showed readiness for the interview SQL test by presenting SQL/database competence as a core capability, not an incidental tool.**

18. **Understood the secure-campus/public-health context, including the need for CTC clearance, confidentiality, accuracy, and responsible handling of high-value scientific data.**

- surveillance, outbreak, pandemic-preparedness, and reference-lab context
