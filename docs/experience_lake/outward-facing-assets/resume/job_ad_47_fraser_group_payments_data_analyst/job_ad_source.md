# Job Description

We're looking for a Payments Data Analyst to join our Digital Risk team at Frasers Group. Reporting into the Payments Data Analytics Manager, you'll play a key role in supporting the analysis and reporting of eCommerce payments and fraud performance.

This is a great opportunity for someone early in their data career who is passionate about analytics, payments, and eCommerce, and keen to develop their skills in a fast-paced, commercial environment.

# What you'll be doing

- Produce, validate and maintain regular reporting and dashboards covering:
  - Payment performance and costs.
  - Fraud and chargeback trends.
  - Operational metrics for the fraud and payments teams.
- Analyse data to identify trends, anomalies and performance issues.
- Investigate unexpected movements in data and support root cause analysis.
- Respond to ad-hoc data requests from Digital Risk, Finance and eCommerce teams.
- Support accurate monitoring of key payment and fraud metrics.
- Work with structured datasets using Excel and SQL-based tools, such as BigQuery.
- Collaborate with internal stakeholders and, on occasion, external payment partners.

# Your insights will help

- Improve payment acceptance rates.
- Reduce costs and chargebacks.
- Strengthen fraud prevention.
- Deliver a smooth and reliable checkout experience for customers.

By providing accurate, timely and insightful analytics, you'll directly support both day-to-day operations and longer-term commercial decision making.

# Qualifications

## Essential

- Basic to intermediate Excel skills, such as lookups, index/match, pivot tables, data manipulation.
- A foundational understanding of SQL and strong willingness to develop further.
- Strong numerical and analytical ability.
- High attention to detail and a structured approach to problem solving.
- Proactive mindset with a willingness to take ownership and learn.
- Ability to work both independently and as part of a team.
- Genuine interest in data, eCommerce or payments.

## Desirable

- Experience working with SQL and structured datasets, such as BigQuery or similar.
- Exposure to BI tools such as Power BI, Looker/Data Studio or Tableau.
- Understanding of online payment journeys, including authorisations, declines, checkout flows.
- Awareness of fraud, chargebacks or loss prevention concepts.
- Experience in retail, eCommerce, finance or other high-transaction environments.
- Understanding of basic data governance and handling of personal data.
