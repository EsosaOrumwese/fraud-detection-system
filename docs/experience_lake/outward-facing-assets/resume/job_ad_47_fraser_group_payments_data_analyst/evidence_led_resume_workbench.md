# Frasers Group — Payments Data Analyst: 3 Screens

## 1. Business / problem screen

Frasers is not primarily hiring a fraud investigator in the Trustpilot sense or a revenue-protection analyst in the Arriva sense. This role sits inside **Digital Risk** and focuses on **eCommerce payments and fraud performance**. The analyst supports reporting and analysis across payment performance, payment costs, fraud, chargebacks, and operational metrics for fraud and payments teams. 

So the CV must prove evidence around:

| Frasers business problem    | What the CV needs to show                                                                                 |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Payment performance         | You can analyse payment success, declines, authorisations, checkout friction, or payment journey issues   |
| Payment costs               | You can track cost movements, payment provider costs, transaction costs, or cost drivers                  |
| Fraud trends                | You can identify fraud patterns, risky behaviour, anomalies, or suspicious transaction/account activity   |
| Chargebacks                 | You can analyse chargeback trends, root causes, rates, volumes, or financial impact                       |
| Operational metrics         | You can maintain reporting for fraud, payments, finance, eCommerce, or digital risk teams                 |
| Unexpected data movements   | You can investigate sudden changes in metrics and explain root causes                                     |
| Checkout experience         | You can connect analysis to smoother customer checkout, fewer declines, or more reliable payment journeys |
| Commercial decision support | You can turn accurate analytics into day-to-day operational and longer-term commercial decisions          |

The strongest business-positioning phrase for Frasers is:

```text
payments, fraud and chargeback analytics for eCommerce risk and checkout performance
```

Not:

```text
fraud modelling and customer behaviour ML
```

That Tesco-style framing is too ML-heavy for this role. Frasers wants a practical payments/fraud analyst who can maintain reports, investigate movement in metrics, and support Digital Risk, Finance and eCommerce decisions.

## 2. Technical / capability screen

Frasers is very concrete about tools and artefacts. They ask for Excel, SQL, structured datasets such as **BigQuery**, and exposure to BI tools such as **Power BI, Looker/Data Studio or Tableau**. They also mention lookups, index/match, pivot tables and data manipulation in Excel. 

Because you have used **SQL, BigQuery, Looker, Power BI, Excel and VBA**, we should state those tools directly where they are relevant.

For Frasers, **dashboards and reports are not supporting evidence — they are central evidence**. The ad explicitly says the role will produce, validate and maintain reporting and dashboards covering payment performance, costs, fraud and chargebacks.

The methodology says the evidence ledger should come **after proof buckets** and before scoring; its purpose is to list candidate evidence from your experience — projects, metrics, tools, datasets, decisions, stakeholders and outputs — so we can later decide what is safe, strong, weak or role-specific.

Yes — absolutely. The platform name is a **positioning label**, not a sacred fixed title.

If **“Customer Behaviour ML Platform”** was our descriptive label rather than an official product name, then we should adapt it to the role’s hiring problem.

For Tesco, “Customer Behaviour ML Platform” made sense because the role was customer analytics, churn, propensity and marketing effectiveness.

For these fraud/payment roles, that title may under-sell the evidence. We can rename the same platform based on the proof we want to foreground, as long as the description and bullets stay truthful.

## Better role-specific platform names

### Trustpilot

Use something like:

```text
Fraud Detection & Behaviour Analytics Platform
```

or:

```text
Platform Behaviour Fraud Detection System
```

This fits Trustpilot because they care about fraudulent behaviour, platform misuse, detection rules, SQL, BigQuery and Looker.

### Arriva

Use:

```text
Fraud Risk & Revenue Protection Analytics Platform
```

or:

```text
Fraud Risk Analytics Platform
```

I would be careful with “Revenue Protection” unless we can support revenue-risk/recovered-value evidence. But for Arriva, that framing is highly relevant.

### Frasers

Use:

```text
Payments, Fraud & Chargeback Analytics Platform
```

only if we genuinely have payment/chargeback evidence.

If not, safer:

```text
Fraud & Customer Risk Analytics Platform
```

or:

```text
Fraud Risk Analytics Platform
```

## The rule

The experience title should answer:

> “What should the recruiter understand this project/platform as, for this role?”

So yes, we can adapt:

```text
Customer Behaviour ML Platform
```

into:

```text
Fraud Detection & Behaviour Analytics Platform
```

or:

```text
Fraud Risk Analytics Platform
```

or:

```text
Payments, Fraud & Chargeback Analytics Platform
```

But we should not adapt it into a domain we cannot defend. For example, we should not call it a **rail revenue protection platform** unless it actually involved rail/revenue protection data.

For Trustpilot, I’d probably use:

```text
Data Scientist
Fraud Detection & Behaviour Analytics Platform — Exeter, UK | May 2025 – Present
```

That keeps the same project truthful while making the role relevance much sharper.
