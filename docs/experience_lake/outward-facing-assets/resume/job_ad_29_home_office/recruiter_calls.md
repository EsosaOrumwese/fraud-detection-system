# Recruiter Calls - Commercial Data Analyst - Home Office

Using the updated job-ad evidence doc, the ideal candidate profile, and the supplied person specification as the source of truth, the recruiter's calls for `Commercial Data Analyst - Home Office` are:

1. Applied data analytics techniques such as data matching, information retrieval, text analysis, network analysis, forecasting, modelling, or trend analysis to support real-world decision-making.

2. Worked with complex operational, commercial, supplier, spend, performance, or similarly governed datasets rather than only simple reporting extracts.

3. Explained the analytical approach taken, why it was appropriate, and what outcome or decision it supported.

4. Collected, cleaned, linked, managed, and assured data from multiple sources, with attention to quality, limitations, security, and information assurance.

5. Understood different data types such as open/public data, administrative data, research data, and operational data, including their appropriate use, limitations, and ethical handling.

6. Understood core data concepts, data structures, database systems, and how data solutions are designed, implemented, maintained, or improved.

7. Contributed to data solutions, data feeds, transformations, or analytical datasets in collaboration with others, including engineering or technical colleagues.

8. Used SQL, Power BI, or comparable tools to manipulate, analyse, visualise, and present data for decision-makers.

9. Developed or maintained dashboards, reports, visualisations, and analytical outputs that supported commercial, operational, compliance, value-for-money, or performance decisions.

10. Worked with users and stakeholders to define data and analytical requirements before building the solution.

11. Ensured analytical solutions were accessible, proportionate, and aligned with user needs rather than over-engineered or purely technical.

12. Communicated complex technical or analytical information clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences, adapting format, language, and detail.

13. Presented analytical or technical work to senior stakeholders with appropriate support, and responded constructively to feedback and questions.

14. Identified, prevented, or resolved data-quality issues and applied quality-assurance principles to analytical work.

15. Showed awareness of data protection, legal, ethical, security, and information-assurance expectations in a government/public-sector data setting.

16. Operated with Civil Service-style behaviours: changing and improving, delivering at pace, constructive stakeholder engagement, and openness to learning new tools and approaches.

The spine of the call here is:

- public-sector commercial analytics and decision support
- complex spend, supplier, performance, compliance, and operational data
- data matching, linking, modelling, forecasting, text/network-style analysis
- SQL / Power BI / comparable tooling
- data quality, assurance, legal and ethical handling
- contribution to data solutions and data feeds with technical colleagues
- accessible stakeholder-led requirements and proportionate solutions
- senior and non-technical communication
- value for money, compliance, operational delivery, and public-service impact
