## Job Summary

Kernow Health CIC is seeking a motivated and detail-oriented Data Analyst to support delivery of high-quality reporting and insights across all Kernow Health CIC services and system partners within Cornwall and the Isle of Scilly.

The postholder will transform complex data into meaningful insights using tools such as Power BI, Excel and related technologies to support clinical, operational and strategic decision-making.

The role involves working closely with colleagues across the organisation and system partners to help improve service performance and patient outcomes.

## Main Duties Of The Job

The role is primarily focused on data analysis and reporting, with opportunities to contribute to wider digital and IT initiatives as part of a collaborative team.

It is suited to an analyst who enjoys problem-solving, stakeholder engagement and developing impactful visualisations.

## About Kernow Health CIC

Kernow Health CIC is a Community Interest Company supporting General Practice and delivering Primary Care Services at scale across Cornwall.

Established in 2011 by a group of Cornwall practices, it delivers several NHS contracts, including:

- Cornwall 111 Integrated Urgent Care Service.
- School Immunisation Programme.
- Cornwall Primary Care Training Hub.
- Special Allocation Scheme.

All profits are reinvested to benefit primary care across Cornwall, supporting sustainable and locally responsive healthcare.

## Job Responsibilities

### Key Duties And Responsibilities

- Develop, maintain and enhance dashboards and reports using Power BI to support performance monitoring and service improvement.
- Analyse complex datasets to identify trends, risks and opportunities, translating findings into clear, actionable insights.
- Respond to scheduled and ad hoc data requests, ensuring outputs are accurate, timely and aligned to stakeholder needs.
- Work collaboratively with colleagues across the organisation and system partners, including ICB and Cornwall and Isles of Scilly system partner organisations, to support consistent and meaningful reporting.
- Support improvement of data quality by identifying anomalies, reconciling outputs and contributing to robust validation processes.
- Extract, manipulate and transform data from a variety of sources using tools such as Excel and Power Query.
- Contribute to development and automation of reporting processes to improve efficiency and reduce manual workload.
- Present data and insights clearly and accessibly to a range of audiences, including non-technical stakeholders.
- Manage and prioritise workload effectively, working independently and as part of a team.
- Contribute to wider digital and IT initiatives within the Data and IT function.
- Provide occasional support with system administration tasks or user queries, as required.
- Participate in service improvement and transformation projects across the organisation.
- Maintain awareness of relevant policies, data governance standards and best practice in data analysis.
- Carry out any other duties that may reasonably be required in line with main duties, as directed by the line manager.

## Person Specification

### Experience - Essential

- Strong working knowledge of Microsoft Excel, including formulas, pivot tables and data manipulation.
- Evidence of continuous professional development or willingness to learn new tools and techniques.
- Experience using business intelligence tools such as Power BI, including data modelling, dashboards and visualisation.
- Experience working in a healthcare, NHS or other regulated environment.

### Experience - Desirable

- Knowledge of data analysis techniques and tools, such as Power Query, DAX and SQL.
- Understanding of data visualisation principles and insight presentation.
- Understanding of data governance, data quality and information security principles.

### Qualifications - Essential

- Degree in a relevant subject, such as Data Analytics, Mathematics, Statistics or Computer Science, or equivalent experience in a data analysis role.

### Qualifications - Desirable

- Relevant professional certifications, such as Microsoft Data Analyst Associate or similar.
