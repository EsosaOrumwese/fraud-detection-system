# Recruiter Calls - Job Ad 39 - ESNEFT

Role:
- `Senior Data Scientist-Analytics Lead`
- `East Suffolk and North Essex NHS Foundation Trust`
- NHS AfC Band `8a`
- Advanced Analytics Team
- NHS Jobs advert, not Civil Service Success Profiles

Using the updated job-ad evidence doc and ideal candidate profile as source material, the recruiter's calls for this role are:

1. **Delivered advanced analytics, machine learning, AI, statistical modelling, and predictive analysis against real operational or clinical challenges.**

2. **Used `R`, `Python`, and `SQL` to analyse complex data, build analytical products, and support operational decision-making.**

3. **Developed supervised and unsupervised machine-learning methods, including regression, decision trees, classification, forecasting, clustering, NLP, or similar approaches.**

4. **Built predictive analyses, visual dashboards, interactive reports, and forecasting outputs that were accessible and useful to operational or clinical users.**

5. **Linked analytics to Trust-level challenges such as waiting-list management, service demand, activity planning, patient care, staff experience, and service improvement.**

6. **Developed or maintained forecasting tools, activity-planning models, benchmarking outputs, or modelling products that supported planning and performance improvement.**

7. **Used `GitHub` or similar source-control workflows for documentation, validation, reproducibility, and quality assurance of analytical projects.**

8. **Managed medium-sized analytical projects from scoping through delivery, including timetable management, delivery risks, quality checks, and agreed outputs.**

9. **Managed or developed analysts/data scientists, including setting expectations, supporting objectives, identifying development needs, and improving team capability.**

10. **Quality assured analytical outputs and took responsibility for accuracy, accessibility, documentation, and usefulness of reports, models, forecasts, and dashboards.**

11. **Worked with operational and clinical colleagues to understand business needs, issues, and drivers, then translated those needs into a pipeline of useful analytical projects.**

12. **Communicated complex, sensitive, or technical analytical information clearly to mixed audiences, including non-technical operational users.**

13. **Used persuasion, influence, negotiation, and professional challenge to help colleagues understand analytics opportunities, risks, limitations, and trade-offs.**

14. **Helped an organisation get value from modern data infrastructure such as `EPR`, `Epic`, `Azure Data Lake`, cloud analytics platforms, or similar analytical environments.**

15. **Used or explored emerging AI and automation tools, including `LLMs`, `Copilot`, `ChatGPT`, or similar tools, to improve analytical workflows or automation.**

16. **Applied strong data-quality, validation, documentation, governance, and risk-management discipline so outputs were trusted and safe for decision-making.**

17. **Worked independently, prioritised competing demands, responded to unexpected requests, and made autonomous analytical judgements.**

18. **Showed leadership behaviours suitable for Band 8a: owning outcomes, developing people, improving services, supporting change, and aligning analytics to organisational strategy.**

19. **Connected analytics to ESNEFT's `Time Matters` philosophy by showing how data science can save staff time, reduce delays, improve patient experience, and support better outcomes.**

20. **Presented as a senior NHS analytics lead: technically advanced, operationally credible, quality-focused, able to manage people and projects, and capable of making analytics useful across a large Trust.**

The spine of the call here is:

- senior NHS advanced analytics leadership
- `R`, `Python`, `SQL`, machine learning, forecasting, dashboards, and AI
- supervised and unsupervised methods, including regression, classification, decision trees, time series, NLP, and LLM-assisted automation
- operational healthcare analytics for waiting lists, service demand, activity planning, patient care, staff experience, and service improvement
- project leadership, portfolio management, delivery planning, and risk escalation
- team development and management of analysts/data scientists
- quality assurance and accountability for analytical outputs
- `GitHub` documentation, validation, reproducibility, and governance
- EPR / `Epic` / `Azure Data Lake` or comparable modern analytics-platform value
- stakeholder influence with operational and clinical colleagues
- alignment to ESNEFT's `Time Matters` strategy

Truth-boundary note:
- Do not claim direct NHS analytics leadership, direct Band 8a-equivalent management, direct line management of analysts, direct Epic EPR experience, direct Azure Data Lake delivery, direct clinical stakeholder ownership, or direct NHS waiting-list/service-demand analytics unless separately evidenced.
- Strongest evidence should be translated from the open-source fraud risk intelligence platform, MSc data-science work, thesis modelling, forecasting outputs, supervised/unsupervised ML knowledge, Git and GitHub reproducibility practice, validation/documentation work, COVID/public-health analysis, and engineering/service experience.
- This is a stretch role. Final materials must sell the strongest advanced analytics and project-ownership evidence without pretending the candidate has already held a senior NHS analytics leadership post.
