# Trust Values Answer - ESNEFT

These values matter in this role because a Senior Data Scientist / Analytics Lead is not just producing analysis and handing it over. The role is also about helping people make sense of evidence in a busy Trust environment, where services are under pressure and where the way information is presented can affect confidence, decisions, and relationships.

For me, optimism is important because good analytics should help teams see what can be improved, not just what is going wrong. Forecasts, models, dashboards, and service analysis should turn uncertainty into practical options, so that teams can plan better, reduce avoidable pressure, and use their time and resources more effectively.

Appreciation is also important because the data does not tell the full story on its own. Clinical and operational colleagues understand the context behind the numbers, including the pressures, exceptions, and practical constraints behind a pattern. An analytics lead needs to listen to that experience and use analysis to support better decisions, not make detached judgements from a spreadsheet or dataset.

Kindness matters because analytical work can sometimes highlight delays, risks, variation, or data-quality problems. Those findings still need to be communicated honestly, but in a way that helps people solve problems rather than feel blamed. In this role, I think that kind of approach would help build trust in the analysis, support adoption, and contribute to a healthier team culture.
