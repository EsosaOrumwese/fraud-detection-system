### Job Purpose Evidence

Thames Water frames this role as a `Data Analyst` role where data skills shape decisions that affect millions of customers. The role sits in `Water Networks Operations` and is focused on turning complex data into clear insight that supports operational change.

The core problem is not just reporting. The role combines large internal and external datasets, database interrogation, data transformation, business liaison, report design, stakeholder engagement, data quality, secure data use, GDPR-aware handling, and clear presentation of analytical insight.

The strongest positioning is therefore:
- operational data analysis
- SQL-led database interrogation and transformation
- Power BI / Tableau reporting
- Python or R scripting as an advantage
- data quality and secure data use
- stakeholder-facing insight for Water Networks Operations

### Responsibilities Evidence

#### 1. Large operational and external datasets

- Work with large datasets from a wide variety of internal and external sources.
- Use environmental data, survey feedback, and operational data.
- Turn complex datasets into practical insight for operational decisions.

#### 2. Database design, interrogation, and transformation

- Design and interrogate databases.
- Carry out data transformations.
- Use analytical thinking to solve business problems.
- Liaise with the business to improve ways of working.

#### 3. Reporting, dashboards, and stakeholder support

- Support the business and stakeholders to design, build, and maintain reports.
- Use dashboarding tools such as `Power BI` or `Tableau`.
- Present insight clearly through reports, dashboards, and presentations.

#### 4. Stakeholder engagement and operational decision support

- Act as a key point of contact.
- Collaborate with colleagues.
- Engage with stakeholders to support objectives.
- Inform colleagues of operational decisions.
- Bridge the gap between business and data.

#### 5. Data quality, security, and GDPR

- Maintain data quality.
- Ensure data is secure.
- Ensure data is properly used in line with `GDPR`.
- Communicate data limitations and analytical outputs clearly.

### Person Specification Evidence

#### 1. Analytical mindset and problem solving

- Positive, curious mindset.
- Natural interest in solving problems with data.
- Confidence working with operational data.
- Willingness and ability to learn new tools and techniques.

#### 2. Data, query, and scripting capability

- Experience with large datasets, databases, or reporting.
- Experience with query languages such as `TSQL` or `ANSI SQL`.
- Experience with scripting languages such as `Python` or `R` is an advantage.
- Familiarity with `R`, `Business Objects`, `Microsoft Azure services`, or `Microsoft Office` is helpful.

#### 3. BI and reporting capability

- Experience with dashboarding software such as `Power BI` or `Tableau`.
- Ability to build clear outputs from operational data.
- Ability to explain insight to different audiences.

#### 4. Communication and stakeholder delivery

- Explain complex information clearly and engagingly.
- Use analytical skills in a business setting.
- Work with and support stakeholders.
- Present insight in a way that generates business value.

#### 5. Education / equivalent experience

- A degree or equivalent experience in an analytical subject such as `Science`, `Maths`, or `Geography` is advantageous but not essential.

### Organisation Context Evidence

- Thames Water describes itself as the UK's largest water and wastewater company.
- The company serves more than `16 million` customers.
- The broader organisational mission is to support customers, communities, people, and the environment.
- The role has a real operational impact because Water Networks Operations decisions affect essential water services.

### Hiring Signal Summary

This job ad is a strong fit for a data analyst CV centred on:
- SQL database interrogation and transformation
- Power BI / Tableau dashboards and report maintenance
- large operational, environmental, and survey datasets
- Python or R scripting as a supporting capability
- data quality, secure data use, and GDPR-aware handling
- business-facing analysis and stakeholder communication
- operational decision support in a high-impact infrastructure environment

This is not a pure data science role and not a finance reporting role. It is an operational analytics role where the CV should show the ability to transform varied datasets into reliable reports, explain operational patterns, improve data quality, and support business decisions through clear insight.
