## Saved Response For Measurement Framework Question

I have not owned end-to-end website tagging implementation on a live public website, but I have built a digital measurement and reporting framework from customer event data. This included creating journey-stage reporting views, recurring digital performance reports, tracking QA checks, validation outputs and performance notes explaining traffic/activity movement, engagement changes, data-quality caveats and optimisation follow-up actions. I am also currently completing Google Analytics training, so I am building the platform-specific tagging and analytics knowledge to complement my hands-on measurement, reporting and QA experience.

## Interview-Safe Framing

The platform should be framed as a **digital journey measurement and tracking QA framework**, not as full ownership of a live Adobe Analytics or Google Analytics website-tagging implementation.

Use this framing if asked directly:

```text
I have not been the sole owner of a live public-website tagging implementation in Adobe Analytics or Google Analytics. However, I have built a digital journey measurement framework from scratch using SQL/BigQuery, Power BI, Looker and Excel/VBA. That involved defining journey-stage metrics, building reusable reporting source tables from customer event and activity data, validating tracking fields for missing values, duplicates, stale refreshes and mismatched totals, and producing recurring digital performance reports with commentary on journey movement, engagement changes and tracking-quality caveats.

So while I would be careful not to overclaim hands-on Adobe tagging ownership, I do have strong transferable experience in the measurement layer around tagging: metric definitions, event-table design, tracking QA, reporting automation and performance interpretation.
```
