# South West Water Water Demand Analyst - 3 Screens

This is the job-ad interpretation stage only: business/problem screen, technical/capability screen, and working-style/stakeholder screen. No proof buckets, evidence ledger, scoring, or bullets yet.

## 1. Business / Problem Screen

This role is about **water demand strategy**, not generic data analysis.

South West Water wants someone who can help develop, monitor and review demand strategy across multiple regions: South West Water, Bristol Water, Bournemouth Water, SES and the Isles of Scilly. The work is tied to **Environment Act requirements**, especially leakage reduction and reductions in household and non-household demand. The job also covers planning horizons from **in-year**, to **AMP**, to **long-term planning up to 50 years**.

| South West Water business problem | What the CV needs to show |
| --- | --- |
| Water demand monitoring | You can analyse water usage / operational trend data over time. |
| Demand forecasting | You can support forecasts, planning assumptions or trend projections across short, medium and long horizons. |
| Leakage and demand reduction | You can identify trends, risks or opportunities linked to demand management, leakage, water efficiency or usage reduction. |
| Regulatory reporting | You can produce reports and documentation that align with business and regulatory requirements. |
| Strategy review | You can contribute evidence for annual strategy reviews or demand-management decisions. |
| Multi-region operational analysis | You can compare demand-related data across regions, teams, time periods or operational units. |
| Water efficiency programmes | You can turn usage data into evidence for demand reduction or efficiency initiatives. |

The CV should eventually feel like:

```text
Water demand / usage data -> trend analysis -> forecast or planning output -> Power BI/report -> risks and recommendations for demand or operational teams
```

Not:

```text
generic dashboarding
commercial customer analytics
promotion targeting
fraud/payments
ML-first data science
```

## 2. Technical / Capability Screen

The technical requirements are not overly tool-heavy, but they are operationally specific. South West Water asks for demand data analysis, forecasting, data extraction/interpretation with data and modelling teams, maintaining data systems/databases, Power BI as desirable, complex dataset interpretation, reports, presentations and technical documentation.

| Data systems / databases | SQL/BigQuery table, database maintenance, reporting table, dashboard source table. |
| Power BI / visualisation | Power BI demand dashboard, operational trend dashboard, KPI report, regional demand view. |

| Data quality | Validation checks, reconciliation workbook, missing/conflicting record checks, report QA process. |

For this role, technical evidence should not be phrased as:

This role sits between strategy, data/modelling teams and operations. South West Water explicitly wants the analyst to collaborate with data and modelling teams, prepare reports and presentations for key stakeholders, contribute to regulatory reporting, act as a link between the demand team and operational teams, and support water efficiency initiatives.

| Working-style requirement | CV evidence to look for later |

| Demand team to operations link | Operational report, regional dashboard, handover summary, field/operations-facing data output. |
| Regulatory awareness | Annual reporting pack, compliance-style report, controlled definitions, audit-ready data tables. |
| Modelling collaboration | Data extract, model input table, forecast support table, assumption summary. |
| Clear interpretation | Trend explanation, variance summary, dashboard commentary, risk/recommendation note. |
| Continuous improvement | Improved database, automated checks, enhanced dashboard, reporting workflow improvement. |
| Trust and reliability | Accurate records, validated reports, traceable calculations, documented assumptions. |

The working-style story should be:

```text
I can work with operational and modelling teams, turn complex usage or operational data into reliable Power BI/reporting outputs, and explain demand trends, risks and recommendations clearly enough for strategy, regulatory and operational review.
```
