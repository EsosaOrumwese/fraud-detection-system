# South West Water Water Demand Analyst - 3 Screens

This is the job-ad interpretation stage only: business/problem screen, technical/capability screen, and working-style/stakeholder screen. No proof buckets, evidence ledger, scoring, or bullets yet.

## 1. Business / Problem Screen

This role is about **water demand strategy**, not generic data analysis.

South West Water wants someone who can help develop, monitor and review demand strategy across multiple regions: South West Water, Bristol Water, Bournemouth Water, SES and the Isles of Scilly. The work is tied to **Environment Act requirements**, especially leakage reduction and reductions in household and non-household demand. The job also covers planning horizons from **in-year**, to **AMP**, to **long-term planning up to 50 years**.

| South West Water business problem | What the CV needs to show |
| --- | --- |
| Water demand monitoring | You can analyse water usage / operational trend data over time. |
| Demand forecasting | You can support forecasts, planning assumptions or trend projections across short, medium and long horizons. |
| Leakage and demand reduction | You can identify trends, risks or opportunities linked to demand management, leakage, water efficiency or usage reduction. |
| Regulatory reporting | You can produce reports and documentation that align with business and regulatory requirements. |
| Strategy review | You can contribute evidence for annual strategy reviews or demand-management decisions. |
| Multi-region operational analysis | You can compare demand-related data across regions, teams, time periods or operational units. |
| Water efficiency programmes | You can turn usage data into evidence for demand reduction or efficiency initiatives. |

The CV should eventually feel like:

```text
Water demand / usage data -> trend analysis -> forecast or planning output -> Power BI/report -> risks and recommendations for demand or operational teams
```

Not:

```text
generic dashboarding
commercial customer analytics
promotion targeting
fraud/payments
ML-first data science
```
