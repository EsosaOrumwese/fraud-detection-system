# Recruiter Calls - Data Analyst - Engineering and Maintenance - Virgin Atlantic

Using the updated job-ad evidence doc plus the ideal candidate profile as the source of truth, the recruiter’s calls for `Data Analyst - Engineering and Maintenance - Virgin Atlantic` are:

1. Analysed Engineering & Maintenance operational data to identify trends, insights, and opportunities that helped teams understand performance and make better decisions.

2. Generated insight that supported process improvement in a live airline engineering and maintenance environment, not detached reporting after the fact.

3. Developed, built, and maintained data models, dashboards, and reports that supported operational decision-making.

4. Delivered reliable and accessible insight across Engineering & Maintenance through user-focused reporting and analytical products.

5. Supported cross-functional projects with timely and accurate analytical input.

6. Worked with stakeholders to understand requirements and translate those requirements into practical data solutions.

7. Contributed to internal tools, workflows, reporting improvements, and wider analytical or digital-solution delivery.

8. Maintained data quality, consistency, accuracy, and governance standards across Engineering & Maintenance analytics.

9. Presented insights clearly and accessibly to mixed audiences, including non-technical stakeholders.

10. Communicated technical outputs clearly enough for operational users to understand and act on them.

11. Worked across analytical, reporting, and data-science tools in an evolving technical environment.

12. Used or had exposure to SQL, Python, R, or similar tools for analytical delivery.

13. Worked with cloud-based analytical platforms such as Databricks, Power Platform, Power BI, Azure, or similar.

14. Built or supported internal digital tools, data products, web-based applications, or workflow improvements.

15. Operated in a commercial or operational environment where analytical outputs affected business-critical processes.

16. Worked collaboratively across teams and showed willingness to keep developing capability in a data-driven environment.

The spine of the call here is:

- engineering and maintenance operational analytics
- trend, opportunity, and process-improvement insight
- data models, dashboards, and reports for decision support
- stakeholder requirement gathering and translation into data solutions
- internal tools, workflows, and reporting improvements
- strong data quality, consistency, and governance discipline
- Power BI / Azure / Databricks / Power Platform cloud-tool fluency
- clear communication to non-technical operational audiences
- analytics embedded in a live airline maintenance environment
