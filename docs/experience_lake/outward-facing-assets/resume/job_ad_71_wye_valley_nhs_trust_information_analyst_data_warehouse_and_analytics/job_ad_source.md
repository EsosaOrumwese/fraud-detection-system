## Job Summary

The advert notes that Wye Valley NHS Trust reserves the right to close the vacancy early once sufficient applications are received.

The purpose of the role is to support and maintain the Information Department's information and business intelligence Data Warehouse, enabling production of key dataset outputs for internal and external reporting.

The role involves development, support and management of reporting requirements and updates to those processes. It will also act as deputy for the System Managers in their absence.

The post is key in working with System Managers on development of systems required for the Information Department to meet statutory reporting requirements.

The postholder will support interfaces and downloads between systems within the Trust to enable data sharing.

The role supplies specialist support and technical expertise to System Managers, the wider Information Team and other departments within Wye Valley Trust on ongoing planning and development of reporting requirements from current and new systems.

## Main Duties Of The Job

- Assist System Managers in design, development, testing, documentation and support of interfaces between Wye Valley systems to improve reporting within the Trust.
- Set up automated reports required by Information Analysts and external stakeholders for regular and ad hoc reporting.
- Identify where work processes can be rationalised and automated.
- Assist in setting up new reports and queries when required.
- Support System Managers in development, management and availability of servers to ensure Trust-wide business-critical information reporting requirements are met.
- Support reporting requirements including Power BI reporting and other SQL Server databases.
- Work with individual users to enable them to produce ad hoc and regular reports via SQL Server, SSRS, Power BI and other tools.
- Help users solve requirements for data analysis.
- Where appropriate, support existing and new systems within the Trust.
- Communicate effectively on detailed information reporting issues with Trust staff at different levels of the organisation.
- Undertake other duties commensurate with the grading and responsibilities/competencies of the post.

## About Wye Valley NHS Trust

Wye Valley NHS Trust is a member of an NHS Foundation Group with South Warwickshire University NHS FT, George Eliot Hospital NHS Trust and Worcestershire Acute NHS Hospitals Trust.

Located on the border with Wales in the shadow of the Black Mountains, the Trust provides acute and community services across Herefordshire and into parts of Powys. It runs Hereford County Hospital and community hospitals in Bromyard, Leominster and Ross-on-Wye.

The Trust describes itself as progressive and forward-looking, with ambitious plans to improve quality and integrate patient pathways through close collaborative working with partners.

More than 3,000 people work for the Trust.
