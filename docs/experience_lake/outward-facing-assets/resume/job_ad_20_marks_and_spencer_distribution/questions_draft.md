I would bring a combination of operational analytics, reporting-product delivery, and practical stakeholder support.

Another strength I would bring is delivery discipline in fast-moving environments. I also place a lot of value on improving how analytics is produced, not just the final report. Finally, I work well with stakeholders. I am comfortable partnering with operational teams, understanding what they need, explaining analysis clearly, and shaping outputs that help them act. I do not see analytics as something done in isolation. I see it as a business-facing service that should make complex operational realities easier to understand and improve.
