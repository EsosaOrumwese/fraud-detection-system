# Recruiter Calls - Data Analyst - Nando's UK & IRE

Using the updated job-ad evidence doc plus the ideal candidate profile as the source of truth, the recruiter’s calls for `Data Analyst - Nando's UK & IRE` are:

1. Wrote and maintained complex SQL models in Dataform or a similar transformation tool, not just queried already-prepared tables.

2. Queried, transformed, and modelled data across a cloud data platform using advanced SQL patterns such as CTEs, window functions, and complex data types.

3. Built and maintained reliable transformation-layer models that served trusted data across downstream pipelines and reporting.

4. Worked with data engineers on the design, build, coding, and testing of data-processing pipelines rather than only consuming engineer-prepared outputs.

5. Built and iterated cloud-based reports and dashboards in Looker or a similar BI tool for reliable self-serve business insight.

6. Created dashboards and reports that gave support teams and restaurants clear, actionable insight into business performance.

7. Translated business questions and requests into clearly scoped, actionable analytical work items.

8. Understood the problem, domain, and business objective before jumping into the technical answer.

9. Worked across varied business domains such as finance, operations, kitchen tech, and stock.

10. Communicated findings clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders, choosing the right medium for the audience.

11. Explained complex findings in plain language and told the story behind the data, including to senior stakeholders.

12. Applied data-quality techniques, wrote tests, identified issues proactively, and communicated data limitations clearly.

13. Documented data models, definitions, and lineage to a production standard.

14. Understood data-governance principles and championed good data-management practice within the team.

15. Used Git as standard practice, including branching, pull requests, peer review, and code review.

16. Identified and implemented opportunities to automate manual data processes and improve ways of working.

17. Used AI-assisted coding tools critically and responsibly, improving speed and quality without blindly accepting outputs.

18. Applied relevant statistical methods where useful and showed judgement about when those methods were appropriate.

19. Contributed to a maturing cloud data environment focused on a single source of accurate, timely data, predictive analytics, and responsible AI exploration.

20. Operated as a full-stack data analyst across modelling, transformation, pipeline collaboration, BI, governance, documentation, and business-facing analytical translation.

The spine of the call here is:

- complex SQL and Dataform / DBT-style transformation modelling
- cloud data platform work, not just dashboard consumption
- collaboration with data engineers on pipelines
- Looker / BI self-serve reporting
- business-problem translation before analysis
- data quality, testing, documentation, lineage, and governance
- Git, peer review, pull requests, and modern analytics-engineering workflow
- automation and responsible AI-assisted coding
- full-stack analytics with product-minded business judgement
