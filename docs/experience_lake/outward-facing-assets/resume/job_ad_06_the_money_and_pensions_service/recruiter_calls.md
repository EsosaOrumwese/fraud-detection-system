This file captures the recruiter-side demand for the Money and Pensions Service `Data Insight Analyst` role so resume and cover-letter writing can answer the call directly rather than reconstructing it from memory.

## Detailed Call List

1. Managed data governance and reporting outputs for a customer-experience and quality function.
2. Worked across systems and evidence sources to turn fragmented data into meaningful reporting and dashboards.
3. Built and maintained meaningful insight dashboards and reports using tools such as Power BI, Qualtrics, Decision Focus, or similar platforms.
4. Produced accurate, timely reporting and dashboards for internal and external stakeholders.
5. Worked confidently with both quantitative and qualitative data, including structured and unstructured evidence.
6. Combined customer voice, QA outputs, operational MI, complaints, and qualitative feedback into one coherent view of performance and risk.
7. Identified trends, risks, gaps, and early warning indicators through data investigation rather than only reporting status.
8. Used root-cause analysis to understand why issues were happening and to support more informed intervention.
9. Supported KPI or framework measurement by setting, monitoring, and tracking measures over time.
10. Supported new data-collection mechanisms, QA measurement systems, pilots, and lightweight data solutions or analytical workflows.
11. Applied validation, cleansing, transformation, documentation, and compliant data-handling standards as part of day-to-day analytics delivery.
12. Set and managed data requirements and maintained structured data sources that were usable, consistent, and compliant.
13. Produced easy-to-digest insight summaries, dashboards, and visualisations for non-technical audiences.
14. Worked cross-functionally with Technology & Change, Data & Analytics, operational teams, service-delivery teams, and other stakeholders to keep data flows accurate and insight usable.
15. Used insight to support evidence-based decision-making and tracked service-improvement impact rather than just producing reports.

## Source-Weighted Reading Note

The source material supports the role as an insight-operations and governance post rather than a standard BI reporting role. The strongest supported lanes are:

- governed reporting outputs and data-requirements stewardship
- mixed-source dashboards and reports from fragmented evidence
- combining quantitative metrics with bounded qualitative-style evidence
- identifying early-warning risk signals and investigating likely drivers
- KPI and framework measurement with directional change tracking
- lightweight evidence-structuring support for downstream analysis
- clear reporting and interpretation for non-technical stakeholders

The source material supports these more carefully:

- literal `Qualtrics` or `Decision Focus` ownership
- raw free-text analytics or `NLP`
- enterprise governance ownership
- strong before-and-after improvement-impact proof

So resume and cover-letter responses should stay strongest on governance, mixed evidence, risk interpretation, KPI/framework support, and stakeholder-ready reporting rather than overstating tooling or enterprise ownership.

## Use Rule

Use the recruiter call as the default response unit.

- If the recruiter reads it as one responsibility, answer it in one bullet where possible.
- Keep governance, mixed evidence, risk, KPI/framework, and non-technical reporting language visible.
- Use tool names plainly when relevant: `Excel`, `SQL`, `Power BI`, `Tableau`, `Python`, `Power Query M`, `DAX`.
- Avoid repo-centric or internal wording.
- Keep wording outward-facing, fast to scan, and commercially legible.
