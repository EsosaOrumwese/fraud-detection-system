# Recruiter Calls - Senior Data Analyst, Guy's and St Thomas' NHS Foundation Trust

As of `2026-04-08`

This file captures the recruiter-side demand for the Guy's and St Thomas' `Senior Data Analyst` role so resume and cover-letter writing can answer the call directly rather than reconstructing it from memory.

## Detailed Call List

1. Led the accurate, timely, and user-friendly provision of workforce intelligence and EDI reporting in a high-accountability NHS environment.
2. Built and maintained EDI dashboards and people-intelligence products that made workforce data usable for decision-making and compliance.
3. Delivered statutory workforce returns such as WRES, WDES, Gender Pay Gap, or similarly regulated reporting obligations.
4. Produced and quality-checked regular, ad hoc, board-facing, and FOI-related workforce reports.
5. Worked confidently across workforce-information systems such as ESR, Oracle HRMS, Discoverer, and related reporting environments.
6. Analysed workforce data across recruitment, progression, turnover, disciplinary, and staff-in-post measures, including protected-characteristic breakdowns.
7. Identified trends, issues, and areas of concern in workforce data and turned them into actionable intelligence for further investigation or action.
8. Handled sensitive workforce information with strong governance discipline, release controls, appropriate exclusions, and pre-release quality checks.
9. Identified data quality issues, understood underlying causes, and improved controls, processes, or compliance monitoring.
10. Developed KPI and compliance-monitoring reports that surfaced data quality issues, governance breaches, or workforce and EDI risks.
11. Planned, coordinated, and delivered workforce-intelligence, EDI, data-quality, or systems projects from initiation through implementation.
12. Managed major reporting cycles and statutory returns as projects, with progress updates for senior stakeholders.
13. Measured the impact of programmes of work rather than only reporting activity.
14. Gathered customer or stakeholder requirements, chose appropriate data and presentation formats, and tailored outputs to user needs.
15. Benchmarked Trust workforce data against other organisations or sector evidence and used that context to strengthen interpretation and recommendations.
16. Built effective relationships across HR, clinical groups, corporate teams, systems teams, and external organisations to strengthen workforce reporting and strategy.
17. Improved workforce-information systems and reporting approaches through user feedback, new technology, automation, or platform improvement.
18. Contributed to wider workforce-systems and information strategy rather than only producing reports.

## Source-Weighted Reading Note

The source material supports the role as a senior workforce-intelligence, EDI, governance, and project-delivery post rather than a generic BI analyst role. The strongest supported lanes are:

- workforce-intelligence and EDI reporting in a high-accountability environment
- EDI dashboard-style outputs, statutory-style equality reporting, and board-ready reporting
- governance-safe handling of sensitive workforce data with pre-release checks
- data-quality issue identification, corrective controls, and compliance-style monitoring
- project-shaped delivery, progress tracking, and bounded impact measurement
- benchmarking, comparator context, feedback-led reporting improvement, and strategy-aware support

The source material supports these more carefully:

- live ESR, Oracle HRMS, or Discoverer estate ownership
- formal statutory submission authority
- whole-Trust governance, audit, PMO, or strategy ownership

So resume and cover-letter responses should stay strongest on workforce reporting, EDI dashboards, statutory reporting, governance, project delivery, and benchmarking without overstating live systems or whole-function ownership.

## Use Rule

Use the recruiter call as the default response unit.

- If the recruiter reads it as one responsibility, answer it in one bullet where possible.
- Keep workforce reporting, EDI dashboards, statutory reporting, governance, project delivery, and benchmarking language visible.
- Use tool names plainly when relevant: `Excel`, `SQL`, `Power BI`, `Tableau`, `Python`, `Power Query M`, `DAX`.
- Avoid repo-centric or internal wording.
- Keep wording outward-facing, fast to scan, and commercially legible.
