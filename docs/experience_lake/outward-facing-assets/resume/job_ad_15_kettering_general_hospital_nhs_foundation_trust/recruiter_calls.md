11. Improved safety-data collection tools, methodologies, systems, audits, and compliance reviews to increase the reliability, timeliness, and usefulness of patient-safety data.
12. Worked with technical reporting tools such as Excel, Power BI, Access, Business Objects, SQL, Qlikview, and SPC-oriented methods as practical delivery tools.
13. Managed a diverse workload in a pressured NHS environment while using analytical judgement to assess robustness, draw conclusions, and support recommendations.
