# Recruiter Calls - Data Analyst, Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency

As of `2026-04-16`

This file captures the recruiter-side demand for the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency `Data Analyst` role so resume and cover-letter writing can answer the call directly rather than reconstructing it from memory.

## Detailed Call List

1. Owned the day-to-day delivery of robust, timely, and insightful management information, dashboards, and reporting rather than only producing occasional reports.
2. Designed and created reporting solutions using Power BI or similar tools, Power Query, Excel, and queried datasets to meet business-user needs.
3. Owned relevant data models, datasets, and the process for developing and maintaining them rather than only consuming existing data outputs.
4. Gathered requirements from different stakeholder groups and translated those needs into report content, format, timescale, data requirements, and reporting logic.
5. Worked with system SMEs to understand source-system business logic and documented tables, elements, business rules, and known data-quality issues.
6. Analysed and interrogated operational data to identify trends, anomalies, hotspots, and early warning signs rather than only displaying metrics.
7. Turned insight from varied operational data into guidance and recommendations for decision makers across business-as-usual, continuous-improvement, and project work.
8. Communicated technical specifications and analytical findings clearly to non-technical business users and tailored communication style to different audiences.
9. Advised, contributed, and challenged stakeholders where necessary while managing expectations and keeping reporting aligned to changing business needs.
10. Used strong Power BI, Power Query, Excel, and database-querying capability as practical delivery tools rather than surface-level tool familiarity.
11. Developed innovative reporting and analysis solutions while still working to agreed standards and collaborating with analysts and data engineers.
12. Supported transparency and governance obligations through publication support, FOI responses, subject access responses, and participation in data-change governance.
13. Managed one or two analysts and contributed to team leadership through guidance, resource planning, prioritisation, and collaboration with other managers.
14. Operated as a service-minded government MI and insight analyst, not just a dashboard builder, with responsibility for turning operational data into useful, understandable, action-oriented insight.

## Source-Weighted Reading Note

The source material supports the role as a government MI, reporting-solution, and operational-insight post rather than a generic dashboard analyst role. The strongest supported lanes are:

- recurring MI ownership and business-user-focused reporting delivery
- dashboard and reporting-solution design using Power BI, Power Query, Excel, and queried datasets
- data-model and dataset maintenance with explicit source assumptions, business rules, and known data-quality issues
- anomaly, trend, hotspot, and early-warning analysis that leads to practical recommendations
- stakeholder requirement gathering, technical explanation, expectation management, and constructive challenge
- transparency, FOI, subject access, and governance-aware delivery
- structured support, prioritisation, and dependable service-minded analytical delivery

The source material supports these more carefully:

- direct line-management ownership at the level described in the ad
- full source-system SME ownership
- formal government publication ownership
- wider team leadership ownership across multiple managers

So resume and cover-letter responses should stay strongest on MI ownership, reporting-solution design, data-model understanding, operational insight, stakeholder challenge, and governance support without overstating formal management or system ownership.

## Use Rule

Use the recruiter call as the default response unit.

- If the recruiter reads it as one responsibility, answer it in one bullet where possible.
- Keep MI, data models, Power BI, Power Query, Excel, anomaly insight, FOI, subject access, and governance language visible.

- Avoid repo-centric or internal wording.
