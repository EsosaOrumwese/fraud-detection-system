# Recruiter Calls - Job Ad 24 - Gloucestershire Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust

Role:
- `Information Analyst - Band 5 x 3`
- `Gloucestershire Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust`

Using the updated job-ad evidence doc plus the ideal candidate profile as the source of truth, the recruiter’s calls for this role are:

1. Provided a focused, high-quality specialist information service for NHS divisions, including regular and ad hoc information flows for internal and external stakeholders.
2. Owned daily, weekly, monthly, and quarterly information flows, ensuring outputs were timely, accurate, and aligned to Trust and external requirements.
3. Supported divisional Surgery or Medicine stakeholders with validated business intelligence that informed service delivery, performance management, and strategic decision-making.
4. Interrogated large volumes of complex operational and clinical data using BI tools, Excel, SQL, and robust analytical or statistical methods.
5. Turned complex NHS data into useful intelligence rather than only producing raw reports or extracts.
6. Implemented or adjusted activity forecasting models, forecast modelling, and activity-planning outputs for delegated information streams.
7. Supported target compliance by monitoring key targets, identifying deviations, highlighting trends, and escalating anomalies or exceptions.
8. Used benchmarking information to strengthen performance analysis and support management decision-making.
9. Proactively reviewed and analysed data, identified key issues, and brought recommendations or corrective-action options to relevant managers.
10. Produced accurate reports, dashboards, scorecards, charts, graphs, tables, and verbal updates for a wide range of audiences.
11. Worked closely with clinicians, consultants, senior managers, nurses, coding teams, audit teams, ICBs, and other stakeholders to understand needs and provide decision-useful information.
12. Managed stakeholder expectations, negotiated deadlines, prioritised conflicting demands, and challenged users professionally where required.
13. Applied robust validation, data-quality checks, and escalation discipline so reporting remained accurate, reliable, and fit for decision-making.
14. Worked within NHS governance, confidentiality, risk-management, and Data Protection expectations when handling operational and clinical information.
15. Contributed to a developing Digital BI function, with openness to new tools, predictive analytics, machine learning, and more mature information-delivery methods.
16. Worked independently under pressure while still supporting the wider BI team through knowledge sharing, training, and positive influence on junior colleagues.

The spine of the call here is:

- divisional NHS BI and information-service delivery
- complex data interrogation using BI tools, Excel, SQL, and analytical methods
- forecasting, activity planning, target compliance, and benchmarking
- proactive anomaly detection and recommendation of corrective action
- validated reporting through dashboards, scorecards, graphs, and tables
- clinician and senior-manager stakeholder support
- data-quality, governance, confidentiality, and risk discipline
- contribution to a maturing Digital BI team with future predictive-analytics direction
