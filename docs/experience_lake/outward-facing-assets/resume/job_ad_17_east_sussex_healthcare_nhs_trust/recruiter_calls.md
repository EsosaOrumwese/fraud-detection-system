# Recruiter Calls - Principal Information Analyst, East Sussex Healthcare NHS Trust

As of `2026-04-16`

This file captures the recruiter-side demand for the East Sussex Healthcare NHS Trust `Principal Information Analyst` role so resume and cover-letter writing can answer the call directly rather than reconstructing it from memory.

## Detailed Call List

1. Led the delivery of high-quality information services across a significant acute-trust portfolio rather than only producing isolated reports.
2. Ensured clinicians and managers had timely, accurate information to support service delivery, operational management, and service improvement.
3. Produced and maintained routine internal reporting and external submissions in line with NHS guidance, protocols, and timetables.
4. Led acute-provider performance analysis across elective, emergency, RTT, waiting-time, patient-flow, front-door, capacity, and demand intelligence.
5. Used specialist NHS dataset, pathway, and definitions knowledge to answer complex operational and performance questions confidently.
6. Developed strategic forecasts, short-, medium-, and long-term activity plans, and demand-and-capacity models rather than only retrospective reporting.
7. Worked with operational and clinical leads to analyse expected demand against capacity and help shape delivery plans.
8. Presented complex analytical findings and their implications clearly to senior stakeholders, including executives, clinicians, and external partners.
9. Guided stakeholders toward action by translating analytical conclusions into clear recommendations and operational direction.
10. Led and managed a small analyst team, including day-to-day supervision, prioritisation, delegation, recruitment, appraisal, training, and development.
11. Handled complex analytical queries and conflicting stakeholder demands while maintaining a high-quality, customer-focused information service.
12. Used strong SQL Server and advanced Excel capability, including query writing and stored procedures, as core delivery tools.
13. Supported the transition from Excel-heavy reporting toward SSRS, Power BI, or other scalable online reporting platforms.
14. Treated data quality, documentation, governance, and submission discipline as core parts of the analytical job.
15. Built working relationships across the Trust and with external stakeholders such as GPs, CCGs, CSUs, NHSI, and other NHS bodies.
16. Contributed to information strategy, self-service reporting culture, policy change, service development, and new analytical methodologies rather than only maintaining current outputs.
