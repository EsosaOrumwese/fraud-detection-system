# Recruiter Calls - Data & Insight Analyst, Claire House

As of `2026-04-05`

This file captures the recruiter-side demand for the Claire House `Data & Insight Analyst` role so resume and cover-letter writing can respond directly to the call rather than reconstructing it from memory.

## Detailed Call List

1. Collected, organised, analysed, and interpreted data from internal and external systems to produce meaningful organisational insight.
2. Built scheduled and ad hoc reports, dashboards, data selections, and interactive visualisations for different organisational users.
3. Produced board packs and senior-leadership reporting that turned organisational data into decision-support.
4. Supported external-facing reporting for funders, bids, tenders, regulatory returns, compliance submissions, and benchmarking.
5. Worked across reporting, dashboards, and the underlying data layer rather than only at the presentation layer.
6. Developed or maintained data infrastructure, data models, queries, and transformation logic that supported efficient processing, storage, retrieval, and reporting.
7. Monitored, audited, and improved data quality, including strengthening controls around data capture and reporting integrity.
8. Identified opportunities to improve efficiency, increase automation, and make reporting workflows more repeatable and scalable.
9. Gathered, understood, and documented business requirements, then translated them into reports, dashboards, or data products.
10. Built strong working relationships with stakeholders of different data-literacy levels and acted as a trusted internal data partner.
11. Worked with BI tools, databases, and coding or transformation tools such as Power BI, SQL, Python, or Power Query M as practical delivery tools.
12. Wrote queries, reports, and presentations clearly enough for leadership, operational stakeholders, and external audiences to use.
13. Supported data governance, IG governance, confidentiality, and responsible handling of organisational data.
14. Collaborated with application-development or systems teams so data initiatives aligned with wider systems change and pipeline integration.
15. Operated as a broad data-and-insight analyst who could combine reporting delivery, stakeholder consulting, data quality, governance support, and light data-platform stewardship.

## Source-Weighted Reading Note

The source material supports a broader role shape than a narrow reporting analyst profile. The strongest supported lanes are:

- scheduled and ad hoc reporting from trusted data
- senior-leadership and external-style oversight reporting
- reusable reporting structures and shared transformation logic
- data quality monitoring, audit packs, and bounded governance support
- workflow improvement and repeatable regeneration of downstream outputs

The source material supports these more carefully:

- broad external reporting ownership across a whole organisation
- full application-development ownership
- full BI-platform or data-estate ownership

So resume and cover-letter responses should stay strongest on reporting delivery, stakeholder-ready outputs, quality controls, workflow improvement, and light reporting-layer stewardship rather than overstating full platform ownership.

## Use Rule

Use the recruiter call as the default response unit.

- If the recruiter reads it as one responsibility, answer it in one bullet where possible.
- Keep recruiter language visible at the front of the response.
- Use tool names plainly when relevant: `Excel`, `SQL`, `Power BI`, `Tableau`, `Python`, `Power Query M`, `DAX`.
- Avoid repo-centric or internal wording.
- Keep wording outward-facing, fast to scan, and commercially legible.
