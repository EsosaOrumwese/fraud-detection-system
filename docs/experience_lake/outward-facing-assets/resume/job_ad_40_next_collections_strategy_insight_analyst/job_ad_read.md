## What The Role Is About

This is a credit-risk and collections-strategy insight role, not a generic analyst role.

The role sits inside NEXT's Credit and Risk team and focuses on understanding credit customers, designing and implementing collections strategies, and supporting customers in financial difficulty.

The role needs analysis that balances:

- fair and empathetic treatment of customers
- effective collections strategy
- regulatory compliance
- business performance and profit
- operational delivery through contact centre, marketing, and technology teams

## Main Hiring Signals

- Produce complex analysis and regular reporting using multiple data sources.
- Work with big datasets, explicitly described as often involving billions of rows.
- Identify meaningful customer, account, and strategy patterns.
- Comment on trends and contribute ideas to solve challenges or improve processes.
- Continuously review collections strategies.
- Drive positive outcomes for customers while remaining compliant with regulations.
- Work across contact centres, marketing, and technology teams.
- Build and present strategy recommendations to internal and external stakeholders.
- Communicate with purpose and respect.

## What They Want The Candidate To Bring

- Analytical experience from an analyst role or highly numerate field.
- Essential large-scale data experience, with the advert explicitly referencing billions of rows.
- Ideally credit-strategy experience.
- Collections or collections-strategy experience is beneficial.
- SQL and Python.
- Visualisation using Excel, Google Sheets, or Power BI.
- Curiosity and problem solving.
- Ability to present insight clearly and gain buy-in for change.

## Best Candidate Positioning From Current Evidence

The strongest positioning is not platform engineering.

The strongest positioning is:

> Risk and customer-strategy analytics using high-volume behavioural and transactional data to identify risk, prioritise review effort, monitor outcomes, and support fair, effective operational decision-making.

The fraud risk intelligence platform should be translated into:

- customer/account behaviour analysis
- transactional risk insight
- risk segmentation and prioritisation
- strategy monitoring
- workload and capacity planning
- operational reporting
- model-supported decisioning
- fair and defensible treatment logic
- regulated-risk thinking

## Main Risk

The advert prefers direct credit strategy and collections experience.

Do not claim direct collections strategy experience unless separately evidenced.

The safer posture is to present the fraud-risk platform as adjacent risk decisioning and customer-risk analytics, then show an understanding of the collections problem:

- customers in financial difficulty
- fairness and empathy
- affordability and vulnerability awareness
- regulatory care
- contact strategy
- arrears outcomes
- roll rates
- cure rates
- treatment effectiveness
- balancing customer outcome with portfolio performance

Do not write this as:

- ML platform work

Write it as:
