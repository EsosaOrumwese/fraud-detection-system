# Recruiter Calls - Job Ad 38 - BPAS

Role:
- `Data Engineer - Business Intelligence`
- `British Pregnancy Advisory Service (BPAS)`
- Healthcare BI data engineering role
- NHS Jobs advert, not Civil Service Success Profiles

Using the updated job-ad evidence doc and ideal candidate profile as source material, the recruiter's calls for this role are:

1. **Built and maintained automated data pipelines that made data available for operational reporting, strategic planning, analytics, dashboards, and future advanced analytics.**

2. **Worked with modern cloud data platforms such as `Microsoft Fabric`, `Azure Data Factory`, `Synapse`, `Lakehouse`, `OneLake`, or comparable cloud data environments.**

3. **Integrated data from multiple source systems, including relational databases, `SQL Server`, flat files, Excel tools, cloud systems, business platforms, and `APIs`.**

4. **Used strong `SQL` skills to extract, join, transform, model, and prepare datasets for reporting and analytical consumption.**

5. **Used `Python` and/or `PySpark` in notebooks, transformation workflows, or pipeline processing rather than only for isolated analysis.**

6. **Modelled data into reliable BI-ready structures, including semantic-model-ready datasets and awareness of star or snowflake schema design.**

7. **Created reusable datasets and integration logic that reduced manual handling and made reporting more consistent across domains.**

8. **Embedded data validation, cleansing, reconciliation, monitoring, error handling, and lineage tracking into data workflows.**

9. **Maintained data quality and data integrity through schema checks, completeness checks, duplicate review, source traceability, and documented assumptions.**

10. **Produced clear technical documentation, source mappings, transformation notes, data dictionaries, and handover material.**

11. **Applied source control and engineering discipline through `Git`, reviewable changes, testing, documentation, and awareness of `Azure DevOps` or `CI/CD`.**

12. **Understood data governance, secure handling, confidentiality, `GDPR`, information governance, metadata, and pseudonymisation considerations for sensitive healthcare data.**

13. **Supported BI analysts and reporting users by making data easier to access, trust, model, and use in dashboards and insight products.**

14. **Translated reporting and analytical needs into practical data-engineering solutions that served operational, clinical, corporate, and planning use cases.**

15. **Communicated pipeline logic, data limitations, quality issues, and transformation decisions clearly to technical and non-technical colleagues.**

16. **Improved automation, reusability, scalability, performance, and reduction of manual reporting or manual data preparation.**

17. **Worked independently, managed changing priorities, and contributed to a maturing BI function through documentation, standards, knowledge sharing, and continuous improvement.**

18. **Showed values fit for BPAS, including support for compassionate, professional, evidence-based reproductive healthcare and a woman's right to choose.**

The spine of the call here is:

- healthcare BI data engineering
- Microsoft Fabric / Azure / Lakehouse / OneLake awareness
- automated pipelines and multi-source integration
- `SQL`, `Python`, and `PySpark` capability
- BI-ready transformation, modelling, and semantic-layer support
- validation, reconciliation, monitoring, lineage, and error handling
- documentation, mappings, data dictionaries, and handover
- secure handling of sensitive healthcare information
- `Git`, Azure DevOps / CI/CD awareness, and maintainable engineering practice
- collaboration with BI, clinical, operational, and corporate teams
- automation and continuous improvement in a growing BI function
- BPAS values alignment and pro-choice mission fit

Truth-boundary note:
- Do not claim direct `Microsoft Fabric`, `OneLake`, `Dynamics 365`, NHS clinical data, patient data, production `PySpark`, Azure DevOps, CI/CD ownership, or healthcare BI employment unless separately evidenced.
- Strongest evidence should be translated from the open-source fraud risk intelligence platform, SQL/Python transformation work, reusable data products, validation/lineage/documentation work, COVID-19 multi-file ingestion and public-health analysis, MSc sensor-data preparation, and confidentiality-aware operational experience.
- Final materials should sound like practical BI data engineering, not generic analytics, not unsupported Microsoft Fabric implementation, and not direct clinical data experience.
