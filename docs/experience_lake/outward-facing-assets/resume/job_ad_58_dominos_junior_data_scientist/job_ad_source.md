# Junior Data Scientist

## About The Role

## Join The World's Leading Pizza Delivery Company

You already know who we are and what we do. Domino's UK & Ireland is the powerhouse behind our exceptional products. We're innovative, dynamic, and laser-focused on delivering outstanding service to our franchisees and customers alike.

Are you at the early stages in your data career? We're looking for a Junior Data Scientist to join us at Domino's. You'll help drive smarter, data-led decisions by cleaning and analysing data, building foundational models, and turning insights into clear, practical outputs that influence how we operate, serve customers, and grow commercially.

Working within a small team, you'll tackle well-defined data problems, support experimentation and reporting, and collaborate with teams across the business, all while developing your technical capability. If you enjoy working independently on technical tasks, is curious and eager to learn, and is motivated by building strong foundations in data science within a supportive, forward-thinking environment we'd love to hear from you.

## What You'll Bring

- Proficiency in SQL and Python, as well as exposure to data visualisation tools like Power BI.
- A degree in a quantitative subject, e.g. Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Science, Engineering, Data Science.
- A solid understanding of data science fundamentals and basic modelling techniques.
- Ability to work confidently with data and explain findings clearly.

## How We Will Support You

- Hands-on learning alongside experienced data professionals.
- Clear development pathways into more stakeholder-facing data scientist roles.
- Exposure to real business problems and meaningful projects.
- A team culture that encourages curiosity, experimentation, and innovation.
- Support to develop confidence in translating technical insights into business value.
