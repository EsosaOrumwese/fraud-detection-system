University College London Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust 
University College London Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust (UCLH) is one of the most 
complex NHS trusts in the UK, serving a large and diverse population. In July 2004, 
we were one of the first NHS trusts to achieve Foundation Trust status. 
We provide academically led  acute and specialist services, to people from the local 
area, from throughout the United Kingdom and overseas.  
Our vision is to deliver top -quality patient care, excellent education and world -class 
research. We provide first-class acute and specialist services across eight sites: 
• University College Hospital (incorporating the Elizabeth Garrett Anderson Wing) 
• National Hospital for Neurology and Neurosurgery  
• Eastman Dental Hospital 
• Royal National Throat, Nose and Ear Hospital  
• Royal London Hospital for Integrated Medicine  
• University College Hospital Macmillan Cancer Centre 
• The Hospital for Tropical Diseases 
We are dedicated to the diagnosis and treatment of many complex illnesses. UCLH 
specialises in women’s health  and the treatment of cancer, cardiac, infection, 
neurological, gastrointestinal and oral disease . It has world class support services 
including critical care, imaging, nuclear medicine and pathology.

Department / Division 
 
Workforce Intelligence plays a critical role in enabling data‑driven decision‑making 
across the organisation by providing robust, accurate and timely workforce information. 
As a centre of excellence, the team delivers national and internal workforce reporting, 
supports statutory requirements, and drives strategic workforce planning. 
We are the Trust’s central hub for managing the integrity of workforce data within ESR, 
including position management, establishment control, cost centre alignment and 
reconciliation with the General Ledger. The team also oversees local data warehouse 
development and provides a responsive ESR support service to internal stakeholders. 
Our department has been recognised for its impact and innovation, with multiple 
Trust‑level award nominations and individual staff awards for excellence. 
Job Purpose  
 
To lead on the development, analysis and delivery of high‑quality workforce 
intelligence that informs strategic and operational decision‑making across the Trust. 
 
A key focus of the role is ensuring robust establishment control, including the 
accuracy, governance and maintenance of ESR position structures and alignment with 
financial systems. 
 
The postholder will act as a subject matter expert in workforce data, ESR, 
establishment management and reporting, contributing to continuous improvement and 
supporting colleagues across the organisation.

Main Duties and Responsibilities 
 
1) Workforce Reporting and Analytics 
• Develop and maintain a dynamic suite of workforce reports using ESR BI, 
Power BI and the local data warehouse. 
• Produce regular and ad ‑hoc management information to support divisional and 
corporate decision‑making. 
• Interpret complex data sets and provide analytical insight to operational and 
clinical teams. 
• Design and deliver standard and bespoke reports, including national returns 
such as ONS surveys. 
• Work with stakeholders to ensure reporting needs are understood and met. 
 
2) Data Quality and Governance 
• Lead on improving workforce data quality to ensure UCLH remains in the top 
quartile nationally. 
• Analyse national data quality reports and ensure corrective action is taken. 
• Maintain high data quality standards to support accurate establishment control. 
• Support the Trust in maintaining and improving its Workforce Validation Engine 
(WoVEN) score. 
 
3) Cross‑Trust Reporting Support 
• Develop workforce dashboards in collaboration with Finance, Information and 
clinical teams. 
• Represent Workforce Intelligence at Trust ‑wide meetings relating to reporting 
and dashboards. 
• Support clinical and corporate teams to devel op their own reporting capability 
and access workforce data appropriately. 
 
• 4) ESR and Workforce Systems Support 
• Provide expert advice to the Workforce Systems Team and wider stakeholders 
on ESR, data structures and workforce processes. 
• Process bulk data uploads into ESR as part of monthly and ad ‑hoc 
requirements. 
• Support responses to Freedom of Information requests, ensuring compliance 
with FOI and Data Protection legislation. 
 
5) Establishment Control and Position Management (Enhanced Section) 
• Lead on the governance, accuracy and maintenance of the Trust’s 
establishment within ESR. 
• Act as the subject matter expert for establishment control, advising managers, 
HRBPs and Finance on correct processes and data standards.

• Ensure all positions, hierarchies and cost centres are correctly configured and 
aligned with the General Ledger. 
• Oversee the creation, amendment and closure of positions in line with Trust 
policies and national guidance. 
• Monitor establishment changes, identify discrepancies and escalate risks or 
trends to senior stakeholders. 
• Support the Workforce Intelligence Business Partner in developing improved 
controls, audit processes and reporting mechanisms relating to establishment 
management.
