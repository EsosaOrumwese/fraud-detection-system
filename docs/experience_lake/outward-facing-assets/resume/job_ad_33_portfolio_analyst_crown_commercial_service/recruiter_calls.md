# Recruiter Calls - Job Ad 33 - Crown Commercial Service

Role:
- `Portfolio Analyst`
- `Crown Commercial Service`
- `Portfolio Management Office`
- `Portfolio & Project Delivery`

Using the updated job-ad evidence doc, the Civil Service Success Profiles capture, and the candidate evidence base as source material, the recruiter's calls for this role are:

1. **Gathered, analysed, and interpreted complex portfolio or operational delivery data to produce trusted insight rather than simply maintaining trackers.**

2. **Produced clear, actionable reports for senior boards or decision makers, helping them understand delivery position, risk, performance, and investment or prioritisation choices.**

3. **Maintained a single version of the truth across complex workstreams, projects, risks, issues, dependencies, outputs, and reporting evidence.**

4. **Monitored performance across priority work, identifying whether delivery, strategic objectives, service value, or expected outcomes were on track.**

5. **Identified emerging risks, issues, dependencies, trends, and blockers that could affect delivery, and translated them into practical next-step insight.**

6. **Used analytical judgement to bring clarity to complexity, especially where information came from multiple sources or had inconsistent definitions, quality, or timeliness.**

7. **Supported governance standards by making reporting evidence accurate, consistent, reviewable, and suitable for assurance or senior decision-making.**

8. **Understood project delivery governance, including the need for clear reporting, risk and issue discipline, dependency awareness, and evidence-led escalation.**

9. **Demonstrated awareness of the project delivery lifecycle and could distinguish between `Agile` and `Waterfall` delivery approaches.**

10. **Worked with or designed outputs for project managers, delivery teams, analysts, senior stakeholders, or governance forums so reporting was usable and decision-relevant.**

11. **Challenged weak, unclear, incomplete, or inconsistent reporting evidence professionally so portfolio information remained reliable.**

12. **Helped optimise resource, workload, or attention by showing where pressure, risk, backlog, or delivery constraint was concentrated.**

13. **Simplified processes and improved ways of working, including using automation, tooling, or responsible AI-assisted methods to improve productivity and quality.**

14. **Communicated complex analytical, risk, or delivery information clearly to mixed audiences, adapting detail and language so people understood what mattered.**

15. **Worked collaboratively, shared information, supported common focus, and built effective working relationships across different roles or functions.**

16. **Demonstrated `Seeing the Big Picture` by linking analysis to strategic objectives, business value, national/public-service outcomes, and stakeholder priorities.**

17. **Demonstrated `Changing and Improving` by identifying improvements, simplifying processes, using technology sensibly, and making change practical for users.**

18. **Demonstrated `Working Together` through joined-up delivery, information sharing, constructive challenge, and support for common outcomes.**

19. **Demonstrated `Communicating and Influencing` through clear, honest, purposeful communication that helped others understand evidence, risks, options, and implications.**

20. **Presented as an analytical governance operator: someone who can turn delivery complexity into trusted portfolio insight for a newly forming PMO.**

The spine of the call here is:

- portfolio data analysis and senior-board reporting
- single version of the truth across complex project and programme information
- PMO governance, assurance, and reporting standards
- risk, issue, dependency, trend, and performance monitoring
- investment, prioritisation, and resource/value decision support
- stakeholder challenge and support for project teams
- project delivery lifecycle awareness, including `Agile` and `Waterfall`
- process simplification, productivity improvement, and responsible tooling/AI use
- HEO behaviours: `Seeing the Big Picture`, `Changing and Improving`, `Working Together`, and `Communicating and Influencing`

Truth-boundary note:
- The candidate should not claim formal PMO employment, senior-board ownership, line management of project managers, or direct Crown Commercial Service/project portfolio experience unless separately evidenced.
- The strongest evidence should be translated from governed analytical platform work, technical risk/issue analysis, reporting discipline, quality controls, documentation, engineering-field escalation discipline, and service-work pressure handling.
- The final application should sound like PMO insight and governance, not generic data analytics and not pure project management.
