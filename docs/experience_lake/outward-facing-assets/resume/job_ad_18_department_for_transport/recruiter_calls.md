## Detailed Call List

1. Produced official-statistics releases end to end, including analysis, commentary, key facts, charts, tables, quality notes, and publication-ready dissemination products.
2. Turned complex rail data into clear, plain-language narratives that non-specialists, policy users, and public audiences could understand and use.
3. Provided statistical advice and analytical support for ministers, policy teams, submissions, briefings, and press messaging rather than only producing internal analysis.
4. Extracted, manipulated, and combined data from multiple existing and new sources to enable more complex analysis and better evidence products.
5. Built and maintained data tools and reporting solutions that made statistical outputs more usable for customers and stakeholders.
6. Used at least one coding language and worked in reproducible analytical pipelines rather than relying only on manual analysis.
7. Used coding to improve data processing, assurance, documentation, and dissemination, and maintained good standards of code management and reproducibility.
8. Performed and documented quality checks on familiar and new data sources and made professional judgements about whether data was fit for purpose.
9. Applied official-statistics quality standards such as the Code of Practice for Statistics and Aqua Book, and communicated caveats, assumptions, and quality implications clearly to users.
10. Worked with external data suppliers and rail stakeholders such as TOCs, ORR, and Great British Railways to improve data quality and understand collection issues at source.
11. Engaged stakeholders proactively to understand requirements, define scope and boundaries, manage expectations, and improve outputs from user feedback.
12. Used data visualisation and dissemination well enough to highlight key messages and make evidence compelling and actionable.
13. Drafted and contributed to externally facing analytical correspondence such as Parliamentary Questions and FOI responses.
14. Delivered analytical dissemination products to deadline with strong workload management, customer focus, and service discipline.
15. Provided line management or structured support to junior staff while planning, prioritising, and distributing tasks to maintain standards.
