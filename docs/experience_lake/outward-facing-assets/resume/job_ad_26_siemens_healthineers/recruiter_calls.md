# Recruiter Calls - Data Analyst - Manufacturing & Digital Enablement - Siemens Healthineers

Using the updated job-ad evidence doc plus the ideal candidate profile as the source of truth, the recruiter’s calls for `Data Analyst - Manufacturing & Digital Enablement - Siemens Healthineers` are:

1. Worked close to manufacturing or supply-chain operations, not remotely from the shop floor, and understood how operational data is generated by systems, sensors, tools, and people.

2. Designed or improved data-collection approaches across manufacturing and supply-chain environments so operational data became reliable, meaningful, and trusted.

3. Helped shape how operational data is captured in the first place rather than only analysing already-curated datasets.

4. Deployed dashboards, KPIs, monitoring tools, and analytics solutions into day-to-day manufacturing workflows.

5. Built or supported performance dashboards covering productivity, quality, yield, scrap, schedule adherence, or similar operational measures.

6. Configured, validated, maintained, or supported data pipelines so operational data remained accurate, consistent, and available.

7. Worked across data integration and pipeline-adjacent tooling such as Azure, SQL, APIs, and SAP data structures.

8. Used Power BI or similar BI and visualisation tools to make manufacturing data usable for operational decision-making.

9. Supported shop-floor users through adoption, training, feedback gathering, and practical help with data tools.

10. Acted as a first point of contact for troubleshooting data and analytics issues, escalating technical issues where needed.

11. Performed root-cause analysis on data-related operational issues and turned findings into practical recommendations.

12. Used data insight to support real shop-floor improvements, continuous improvement, and better operational decision-making.

13. Maintained clear documentation for data flows, KPI definitions, and user guidance.

14. Contributed to KPI and reporting standardisation across sites or teams.

15. Translated complex operational data into clear, practical insights for technical and non-technical stakeholders.

16. Worked collaboratively across manufacturing, enablement, and IT teams to support future digital transformation, advanced analytics, and automation.

The spine of the call here is:

- manufacturing and supply-chain data close to the shop floor
- trusted operational data capture from systems, sensors, tools, and physical processes
- dashboards, KPIs, and monitoring tools embedded into live workflows
- Power BI plus Azure / SQL / APIs / SAP integration awareness
- pipeline validation and operational data reliability
- shop-floor user support, adoption, training, and troubleshooting
- root-cause analysis and practical improvement recommendations
- documentation, KPI standardisation, and future-factory digital enablement
