# Recruiter Calls - R&D Data Analyst, Guy's and St Thomas' NHS Foundation Trust

As of `2026-04-14`

This file captures the recruiter-side demand for the Guy's and St Thomas' NHS Foundation Trust `R&D Data Analyst` role so resume and cover-letter writing can answer the call directly rather than reconstructing it from memory.

## Detailed Call List

1. Owned or supported a specialist research-management system such as EDGE, including implementation, maintenance, troubleshooting, and local expert support.
2. Ran and improved a research-performance data environment using databases, dashboards, and reporting structures rather than only producing one-off reports.
3. Integrated data from multiple research or operational repositories and turned it into timely information for internal decision-making.
4. Delivered high-accountability internal and external research-performance reporting, including mandatory national returns and senior internal reporting.
5. Produced quarterly, annual, ad hoc, board-level, and external-body reports for research performance rather than only local operational outputs.
6. Analysed complex trends across multiple data streams and forecast short-term and long-term implications.
7. Used analytical judgement to decide when research data was fit for purpose for reporting and analysis.
8. Maintained data quality through quality control, audit, consistency checks, issue resolution, and recommendations for improvement.
9. Worked within mandatory reporting requirements, NHS data standards, confidentiality rules, Data Protection, Caldicott, and other research-governance expectations.
10. Provided frontline advisory support to users of the research-management system and helped staff resolve data and reporting issues.
11. Delivered Trust-wide training, maintained training materials and procedural documentation, and supported staff to use research data and systems consistently.
12. Managed workload against recurring deadlines and new data requests while troubleshooting problems logically and independently.
13. Contributed to policy, process, and team capability through line management, staff development, and input into data-collection, storage, and management practices.
14. Worked as a research-performance and research-governance analyst, not just a generic BI analyst or research assistant.

## Source-Weighted Reading Note

The source material supports the role as a research-performance, research-management-system, and governance-aware analytics post rather than a generic BI analyst role. The strongest supported lanes are:

- specialist-system-backed research-performance reporting from one prepared reporting base
- mandatory-return and dashboard outputs from the same controlled reporting lane
- explicit data-quality, audit, and governance-safe release support
- multi-source trend analysis, implication reading, and fit-for-purpose judgement
- user advisory support, training, documentation, and data-consistency enablement

The source material supports these more carefully:

- live EDGE ownership
- live CPMS integration ownership
- direct national-submission authority
- a full Trust governance office, audit office, or enterprise training function

So resume and cover-letter responses should stay strongest on research-system support, national-style reporting, data quality, governance, fit-for-analysis judgement, and user enablement without overstating live system or whole-function ownership.
