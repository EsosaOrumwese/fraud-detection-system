# Prelim Response - Homes England Senior Data Engineer

Question:

> Tell us why you want to be a Senior Data Engineer at Homes England? (Max 400 words)

## How the Question Should Be Answered

This is phrased as a motivation question, but it should still carry scoreable evidence against the Senior Data Engineer person specification.

The answer must show:

- why Homes England's public-purpose data work matters to the candidate
- why the Senior Data Engineer role fits the candidate's evidence base
- evidence of cloud-aware data-platform thinking
- evidence of SQL/Python pipeline and transformation capability
- evidence of scalable data modelling, curated layers, lineage, and documentation
- evidence of data quality, governance, security, testing, and repeatability
- evidence of ambiguity management and business-to-technical translation
- evidence of insight presentation through Power BI/Tableau-style outputs

## Truth Boundaries

Do not claim:

- direct paid Azure platform employment
- direct production PySpark ownership
- direct Homes England domain experience
- senior stakeholder management
- line management or formal colleague management
- commercial negotiation experience

Use:

- Azure data-platform awareness
- rapid PySpark adoption
- public-purpose motivation
- production-shaped data-platform evidence
- stakeholder-readable documentation
- operational, commercial, and public-purpose impact awareness

## Raw Answer Material

I want to be a Senior Data Engineer at Homes England because the role connects strong data-platform engineering with decisions that affect housing delivery, investment, planning, and communities. That public-purpose link matters to me. I am interested in data engineering where reliable pipelines, governed datasets, and clear data models help an organisation make better decisions, not just where code runs successfully in isolation.

My closest evidence is from building production-shaped data-platform work around a Fraud Risk Intelligence Platform [Open-source]. I used SQL and Python to create reusable analytical data products across events, decisions, entity histories, labels, case movement, controls, and reporting evidence. The important part was not only producing outputs, but designing linked data models with defined grains, documented keys, controlled joins, lineage, and curated structures so downstream analysis could be consistent and repeatable. This is the type of platform thinking I would bring to Homes England: data pipelines and models that are reliable enough to be reused by analysts and decision makers.

I am also attracted to the role because it places data quality, governance, and engineering standards at the centre of delivery. My work has included source traceability, schema checks, completeness checks, reconciliation, duplicate review, leakage controls, documented assumptions, and root-cause investigation. I have also used modular Python/SQL workflows, Git-based traceability, testing, run documentation, and handover notes. Those habits matter in a senior data engineering role because a shared data platform needs trust, maintainability, and clear ownership of how data is transformed and served.

The role also fits how I approach ambiguity. I have had to decide what the data could answer, which datasets mattered, what checks were required before outputs could be trusted, and how technical results should be translated for planning or operational use. For example, I translated modelling and prioritisation outputs into planning reliability, workload efficiency, and operational risk implications rather than leaving them as technical metrics. Alongside Python and SQL, I would bring Power BI/Tableau reporting awareness, Azure data-platform awareness, and readiness to extend further into PySpark where required.

What attracts me most is the opportunity to contribute to a shared platform where engineering quality, governance, public-purpose impact, and practical usability all matter. I would bring a careful, evidence-led approach to building data products that help Homes England become more data-led.
