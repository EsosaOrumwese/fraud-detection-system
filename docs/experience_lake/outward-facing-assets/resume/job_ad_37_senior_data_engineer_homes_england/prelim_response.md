# Prelim Response - Homes England Senior Data Engineer

Question:

> Tell us why you want to be a Senior Data Engineer at Homes England? (Max 400 words)

## How the Question Should Be Answered

This is phrased as a motivation question, but it should still carry scoreable evidence against the Senior Data Engineer person specification.

The answer must show:

- why Homes England's public-purpose data work matters to the candidate
- why the Senior Data Engineer role fits the candidate's evidence base
- evidence of cloud-aware data-platform thinking
- evidence of SQL/Python pipeline and transformation capability
- evidence of scalable data modelling, curated layers, lineage, and documentation
- evidence of data quality, governance, security, testing, and repeatability
- evidence of ambiguity management and business-to-technical translation
- evidence of insight presentation through Power BI/Tableau-style outputs

## Truth Boundaries

Do not claim:

- direct paid Azure platform employment
- direct production PySpark ownership
- direct Homes England domain experience
- senior stakeholder management
- line management or formal colleague management
- commercial negotiation experience

Use:

- Azure data-platform awareness
- rapid PySpark adoption
- public-purpose motivation
- production-shaped data-platform evidence
- stakeholder-readable documentation
- operational, commercial, and public-purpose impact awareness

## Raw Answer Material

I want to be a Senior Data Engineer at Homes England because the role connects technical data-platform work with decisions that affect housing delivery, investment, planning, and communities. The work appeals to me because reliable data engineering in this setting is not just about moving data; it is about creating trusted foundations that help a public-purpose organisation understand delivery, monitor risk, and make better decisions from complex information.

My strongest fit is the way I have built production-shaped data-platform evidence around reusable analytical datasets rather than isolated analysis. On the Fraud Risk Intelligence Platform [Open-source], I used SQL and Python to build analytical data products across events, decisions, entity histories, labels, case movement, controls, and reporting evidence. I designed linked data models with defined grains, documented keys, controlled joins, lineage, and curated structures so downstream analysis could depend on consistent datasets. That is the kind of platform thinking I would bring to Homes England: data pipelines and models that are reliable, reusable, and understandable.

I am also drawn to the quality and governance expectations of the role. My data work has involved repeatable transformation and validation workflows covering source traceability, schema checks, completeness, reconciliation, duplicate review, leakage controls, documented assumptions, and root-cause investigation. I have applied software-engineering discipline through modular Python/SQL workflows, Git-based traceability, testing, run documentation, and handover notes that support reuse. This matters because data platforms only create value when users can trust the data, understand its lineage, and reproduce the process behind it.

The role also fits how I like to work through ambiguity. I have had to decide what data could answer, which datasets mattered, what checks were required, and how outputs should be presented for decision use. For example, I translated modelling and prioritisation outputs into planning reliability, workload efficiency, and operational risk implications rather than leaving them as technical metrics. I would bring that same combination of Python, SQL, Power BI/Tableau-style communication, Azure data-platform awareness, and rapid PySpark learning to Homes England's data environment.

What attracts me most is the chance to contribute to a shared data platform where engineering quality, public-purpose impact, governance, and practical usability all matter. I would bring a careful, evidence-led, quality-focused approach to building data products that help Homes England become more data-led.
