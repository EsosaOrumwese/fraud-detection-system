# Sainsbury’s — 3 Screens

## 1. Business / problem screen

This is a **commercial analytics role for pricing and promotions**, not an operational reporting role like Thames and not a payments/fraud role like Frasers.

Sainsbury’s is telling us the commercial scale upfront: millions of customers, the UK’s largest loyalty scheme, digital platforms, and over **1.2 billion transactions annually**. The analyst sits in **Price & Promotion Analytics** and is expected to shape pricing and promotion decisions across the business. 

| Sainsbury’s business problem | What the CV needs to show                                                                                                  |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pricing decisions            | You can analyse price changes, customer response, sales movement, margin trade-offs, basket behaviour or value perception. |
| Promotion decisions          | You can measure promotion performance, uplift, offer targeting, campaign response, redemption or post-promotion effects.   |
| Commercial performance       | You can connect data to sales, margin, conversion, basket size, customer value or category-level outcomes.                 |
| Large retail/customer data   | You can work with high-volume transaction, loyalty, customer, product or behavioural datasets.                             |
| Strategic decision-making    | You can compare pricing/promotion options and show which one performs better against defined measures.                     |
| Customer understanding       | You can use customer segments, behavioural signals or loyalty-style data to explain who responds to what.                  |
| Experimentation / learning   | You can test assumptions, compare baselines and turn analysis into repeatable pricing or promotion evidence.               |

The strongest positioning phrase is:

```text
pricing, promotion and customer analytics for commercial decision-making
```

Not:

```text
operational reporting and data quality
```

Not:

```text
payments, fraud and chargebacks
```

Not:

```text
generic customer behaviour ML
```

The CV needs to feel like someone who can analyse **price, promotion, customer response, transactions, dashboards, statistical models and commercial recommendations**.
