## Job summary

Specialist Information Analyst - MHLD Division

An exciting opportunity has arisen for a Specialist Information Analyst to join the Analysis Team, with a dedicated focus on mental health data, analysis and reporting.

This role will support a wide range of stakeholders across mental health services, providing high-quality analysis and insight to inform operational delivery, service improvement and strategic decision-making. The post holder will work with a high degree of autonomy and will be expected to proactively identify opportunities where data and analysis can add value.

The Division will be embarking on the implementation of a new Electronic Health Record (EHR) within the next 1-2 years, and the development of effective, reliable reporting will be pivotal to supporting this programme of work. The successful candidate will play a key role in shaping the reporting approach ahead of and during this transformation.

Previous experience of the NHS is desirable but not essential; above all you must be comfortable working with numbers and people, a logical and analytical thinker, and highly proficient in the use of the Microsoft suite of products along with analytical systems such as R or Python. You will share our commitment for high quality information to support organisational decision making.

### Main duties of the job

The post holder will:

- Lead on the analysis, interpretation and reporting of mental health data, ensuring outputs are accurate, timely and meaningful.
- Design, develop and maintain a range of routine and ad-hoc reports and dashboards to support operational and strategic requirements.
- Work closely with a large and diverse group of stakeholders, including operational leads, clinicians and senior managers, to understand and meet their data and information needs.
- Use SQL and other analytical tools to extract, manipulate and analyse data from multiple sources.
- Apply appropriate analysis techniques, including trend analysis, performance monitoring and data quality assessment.
- Support services to better understand their data, highlighting risks, opportunities and areas for improvement.
- Contribute to the development of reporting and analytics in preparation for the implementation of the new EHR.
- Manage a varied workload with competing priorities, working independently and using judgement to plan and deliver work effectively.
- Promote good data quality and consistent use of information across mental health services.

The ability to speak Welsh is desirable for this post; Welsh and/or English speakers are equally welcome to apply.

### Job Purpose

The purpose of the role is to provide specialist information support to CPGs and corporate departments across the organisation. The post holder will be a specialist in their particular area and will work with operational managers and their staff to develop detailed understanding of the organisation's information requirements.

The post holder will lead discrete pieces of analytical work and be responsible for advising on specialist information issues. The post holder will support a range of organisational and CPG projects, providing specialist information advice and analysis.

The post holder will be responsible for developing new reporting tools and techniques and ensuring that the department is maximising its use of appropriate technologies to ensure the provision of a high quality information service.

### Duties and Responsibilities

#### Communication

- Communicate complex statistical techniques to non-Informatics staff, ensuring that complex messages are fully understood, often where there are barriers to understanding and persuasive skills are required.
- Deliver formal presentations on complex information, combining numerical findings with a narrative and ensuring that findings are placed in context for the audience.
- Train staff in the understanding of complex statistical techniques, ensuring that the organisation has a broader understanding of numerical concepts and is comfortable reading statistical reports.
- Represent the organisation at national information workshops, ensuring that the particular requirements of the organisation are reflected in national developments.
- Regularly provide expert advice in a range of settings, ensuring that senior managers and clinicians are constantly briefed on key findings, and develop working relationships with these individuals across formal and informal situations.
- Act as the Information Lead for a CPG and support senior analysts in the provision of specialist analysis across the organisation.
- Develop a strong and fluid two-way communication process with CPGs that ensures the Information Service understands operational requirements and operational staff are more aware of what information and analysis is available.
- Develop the scope of report writing within the department, using numbers to build an argument and coherently integrating objective analysis with more subjective findings.

#### Information Analysis

- Analyse and interpret complex data, where there are often a number of possibilities, to produce detailed, concise reports for clinicians and managers.
- Use complex statistical techniques to translate abstract and bespoke datasets into meaningful reports.
- Work with clinicians and managers to ensure that findings and agreed outcomes from reports are taken forward and implemented.
- Undertake advanced statistical procedures such as regression analysis, confidence intervals, statistical process control and sensitivity analysis.
- Undertake complex geographical analysis on a range of datasets using relevant GIS and mapping software.
- Undertake complex pathway analysis, building a greater understanding of how patients flow through the system rather than analysing discrete elements of the service in isolation.

#### Information Development

- Develop new cubes, dashboards, scorecards and reports using relevant Microsoft business objects.
- Keep abreast of the latest developments and industry best practice in relation to development tools.
- Review and quality assure new and existing elements of data infrastructure such as stored procedures, SQL scripts and reports.
- Ensure that technical documentation is up to date at all times, and that complex processes around data extraction, loading and migration are fully documented in a simple, transparent and understandable manner.
- Develop and validate database schemas using nationally recognised data dictionary standards.
- Work closely with the Data Quality Team to ensure operational and system data quality issues are identified, tracked and addressed accordingly.
- Ensure data schemas, data dictionaries, data dependencies, SharePoint sites and all related feeds are documented and maintained to a professional standard.
- Ensure consistency of operation across all content delivery systems.
- Develop improved reporting techniques and methods, including exception reporting, trend analysis, trajectory planning, deviations from normal state, executive-level analysis, online access and drill-down access to the data warehouse.

#### Information Reporting

- Produce a range of regular reports to meet CPGs' ongoing requirements.
- Develop the scope of information reporting, in conjunction with operational staff, to ensure it continues to reflect operational priorities.
- Develop flexible reporting systems that can be amended to reflect current requirements and support self-service opportunities for operational staff.
- Respond to ad-hoc requirements of CPGs by providing the necessary information, and develop ad-hoc reports into regular reports when appropriate.
- Review the effectiveness of reporting systems, providing advice and guidance on developments required to continue meeting the evolving needs of the organisation.
- Develop and maximise the use of complex technologies to create user-friendly reporting interfaces, allowing users to move between required levels of information in a seamless manner.
- Work proactively with senior operational colleagues across the Trust to help define information requirements that will help them understand the nature of their services and the factors affecting delivery.
- Work closely with colleagues to ensure they make effective use of available information and analyses, giving support to understand and interpret information through coaching, training and similar approaches.
- Co-ordinate production of information for national submissions, ensuring they are appropriate for sign-off.
- Lead on specific areas of work such as capacity planning, RTT or statutory returns, rotating over time to ensure a broad understanding of all areas.

#### Information Standards

- Critically analyse data and flag data irregularities and inconsistencies to service managers and operational staff.
- Delve into known data irregularities to clarify understanding and resolve issues where possible.
- Develop and maintain data capture methods, processes and validations to ensure data consistency and quality and, where possible, provide automatic capture.
- Develop a reporting assurance framework that provides confidence to the organisation that reports undergo regular quality assessment.

#### Planning

- Contribute to the planning and formulation of the Health Board's Information Strategy.
- Plan complex programmes of work such as the development and implementation of new reporting tools, adjusting plans as necessary to ensure CPG requirements are fully reflected.
- Develop a planning template across the department for managing complex projects, ensuring a consistent and robust mechanism for delivering specified requirements.

#### Information Resources

- Design information systems to support operational staff within CPGs to collect information, ensuring they comply with relevant data structures and support future analysis of the data.
- Continually adapt data collection systems to collect further information as required and ensure flexibility is designed into systems to facilitate future adaptations.
- Design systems for dissemination of data as well as collection, ensuring that appropriate technical linkage is pulled through effectively from back-end into front-end reporting mechanisms.
- Manage various information systems on an ongoing basis, ensuring regular updates and ensuring results are published regularly.

#### Human Resources

- Co-ordinate the work of more junior staff in the department, allocating tasks and supervising specific areas of work.
- Deliver training programmes to staff within the department, spreading learning and understanding of complex techniques and providing opportunities for all staff to develop their skills.
- Deputise for more senior staff within the department, providing support and guidance to staff in their absence.

#### Policy

- Implement a range of departmental procedures and policies, ensuring compliance from staff at all levels.
- Develop and maintain a departmental policy documentation process, ensuring appropriate review of policies takes place at regular intervals and that electronic and paper documentation remain up to date.
- Following review of policies and procedures, propose necessary changes to ensure they remain fit for purpose and continue to serve the organisation and department's customers as intended.
- Review National Data Set Change Notices (DSCNs) and propose changes to local practice to adhere to these, impacting how data is collected throughout the organisation.

#### Research / Development

- Regularly undertake audits of data quality within the Health Board to ascertain the robustness of data used for reporting against key targets.
- Undertake audits of surgical practice within the Health Board, benchmarking consultants against their peers.
- Regularly undertake RTT audits to provide assurance on the probity and robustness of data held within various systems.

#### Financial Responsibilities

- Work closely with CPGs to develop detailed analysis of their activity and efficiency performance, supporting the development of plans to remain within costs.
- Work closely with management accountants to ensure the accuracy of costings through robust analysis of data quality.
- Support the finance department in identifying non-contracted activity, ensuring timely and accurate billing to relevant PCTs.

#### Freedom to Act

- Work to achieve agreed objectives and have the freedom to execute duties in their own way, acting within broad professional policies.
- Lead various areas of work and regularly advise organisational staff on information issues without reference to the line manager.

#### General

- Plan workload in order to comply with all routine local and national requirements while being able to respond to regular ad-hoc requirements in a timely manner.
- Use advanced keyboard skills to manipulate large amounts of data at high speed and with great accuracy, including importing and exporting data between Microsoft Office packages to provide meaningful information for end users.
- Keep abreast of the latest guidance and legislation and ensure implementation within the post holder's own work area, for example Data Set Change Notices.
- Use a computer for long periods of time, often for a whole working session.
- Concentrate for long periods while undertaking complex pieces of analysis where attention to detail is essential.
- Be fully conversant and compliant with the Data Protection Act, Freedom of Information Act and the Health Board's IT Security Policy.
- Undertake any other reasonable duties as required.

#### Physical Effort

**Nature and frequency:** Daily.

The post is office based, with a requirement to travel to meetings across North Wales throughout the week. When in the office, there will be continuous use of the PC to undertake analysis.

#### Mental Effort

**Nature and frequency:** Daily.

There is a requirement for prolonged concentration on a daily basis when undertaking complex analysis on a range of large datasets. When working with datasets that can include millions of records, there is a requirement for attention to detail and accuracy, particularly when linking patient records across datasets to ensure the accuracy and integrity of records remain.

The ability to concentrate in this way is core to the duties of the post and more often than not required when under pressure to meet deadlines or in order to analyse information. Prolonged concentration is also required to construct database queries and complex spreadsheet formulae that can manipulate millions of records of data accurately. This occurs daily within a deadline-driven environment.

#### Emotional Effort

**Nature and frequency:** Monthly.

As part of the Information reporting function of the department, the post holder would deal with information concerning death, injuries and serious illness. The post holder would also be involved in the audit of any mortalities which an external benchmarking organisation deemed unexpected.

#### Working Conditions

**Nature and frequency:** Daily.

The post holder will be required to spend long periods of time using VDU equipment to produce database queries, complex formulae in spreadsheets, analyse data, write reports and deal with electronic correspondence.

The post holder is required to travel between Health Board locations across North Wales.
