## Evidence Item 1 - Line-by-Line Candidate Screen Against Criteria

### Context

This is a precursor candidate screen, not a supporting statement and not an interview answer. The question is:

> "If Eric were asked, line by line, how do you meet this criterion, what would his simple, concrete answer be?"

The role is scored mainly through the advert, person specification and full JD: mental health reporting, SQL and analytical tools, dashboards, data quality, statistical methods, EHR reporting preparation, stakeholder communication, NHS data definitions, Data Protection, FOI, workload pressure and Band 6-level autonomy. The full JD also adds advanced statistical analysis, data dictionaries, SQL scripts, dashboard development, report assurance, pathway analysis, national submissions, staff training and information standards. BCUHB's current values are **Compassion, Openness and Respect**. ([Betsi Cadwaladr University Health Board][1])
