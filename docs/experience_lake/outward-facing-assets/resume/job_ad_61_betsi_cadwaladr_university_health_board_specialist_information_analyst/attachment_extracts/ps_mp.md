### Qualifications / Knowledge

#### Essential

- Educated to degree level or equivalent relevant experience.
- Post-graduate diploma in Informatics / Statistical subject or equivalent relevant experience.
- Specialist knowledge of a range of statistical techniques, e.g. SPC, standard deviation, confidence intervals, ANOVA.

#### Desirable

- Membership of an Informatics professional body, e.g. British Computer Society or UKCHIP.
- Management experience.
- A Level Mathematics.

### Experience

#### Essential

- Significant experience in an analytical role across a number of areas.
- Specialist knowledge and understanding of the Health Board's computerised administration systems.
- Experience of working with a range of statistics with a high degree of numeracy.
- Specialist knowledge of Microsoft Office Suite products, particularly Excel and Access.
- Expertise in translating all National Minimum Datasets / abstract data into comprehensible and meaningful information for end users, i.e. key decision makers.
- Expert knowledge of National Data Dictionary items and NHS information codes.
- Ability to comprehend and work within the Health Board's policies of Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

#### Physical Effort

**Nature and frequency:** Daily.

The post is office based, with a requirement to travel to meetings across North Wales throughout the week. When in the office, there will be continuous use of the PC to undertake analysis.

#### Mental Effort

**Nature and frequency:** Daily.

There is a requirement for prolonged concentration on a daily basis when undertaking complex analysis on a range of large datasets. When working with datasets that can include millions of records, there is a requirement for attention to detail and accuracy, particularly when linking patient records across datasets to ensure the accuracy and integrity of records remain.

The ability to concentrate in this way is core to the duties of the post and more often than not required when under pressure to meet deadlines or in order to analyse information. Prolonged concentration is also required to construct database queries and complex spreadsheet formulae that can manipulate millions of records of data accurately. This occurs daily within a deadline-driven environment.

#### Emotional Effort

**Nature and frequency:** Monthly.

As part of the Information reporting function of the department, the post holder would deal with information concerning death, injuries and serious illness. The post holder would also be involved in the audit of any mortalities which an external benchmarking organisation deemed unexpected.

#### Working Conditions

**Nature and frequency:** Daily.

The post holder will be required to spend long periods of time using VDU equipment to produce database queries, complex formulae in spreadsheets, analyse data, write reports and deal with electronic correspondence.

The post holder is required to travel between Health Board locations across North Wales.
