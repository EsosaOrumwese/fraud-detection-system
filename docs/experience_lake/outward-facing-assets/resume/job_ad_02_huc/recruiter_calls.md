## 1. Detailed Call List

1. Analysed data from multiple sources to measure service performance and support operational reporting.
2. Owned recurring service-line reporting and delivered daily, weekly, and monthly reports accurately and on time.
3. Completed contractual reports for a service line and supported reporting against local and national performance requirements.
4. Worked with large, multi-source operational datasets and reconciled differences across systems before producing outputs.
5. Detected anomalies, investigated discrepancies, and worked with BI or operational colleagues to resolve data issues.
6. Tracked performance trends and used data to highlight risks, issues, or pressure points in service delivery.
7. Used reporting and analysis not just to describe performance, but to support operational delivery and strategic decision-making.
8. Presented analytical findings clearly to non-technical audiences, including managers, commissioners, senior leaders, or board-level stakeholders.
9. Used data storytelling to explain what changed, why it mattered, and what stakeholders should take from it.
10. Built or supported decision-support outputs using Excel first, with Power BI and ideally SQL as practical working tools.
11. Scoped, developed, and tailored reporting or decision-support tools to user and service requirements.
12. Created and maintained reporting documentation such as how-to guides, mappings, report notes, or schema definitions to improve clarity and repeatability.
13. Engaged confidently with service stakeholders to understand data content, service requirements, and reporting needs.
14. Communicated sensitive or complex performance information with enough tact and diplomacy to influence decisions.
15. Helped strengthen a data-driven culture by making reporting usable, understandable, and relevant to day-to-day service management.
