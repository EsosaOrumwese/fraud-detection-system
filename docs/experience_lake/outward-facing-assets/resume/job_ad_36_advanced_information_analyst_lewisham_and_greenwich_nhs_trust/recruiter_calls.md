# Recruiter Calls - Job Ad 36 - Lewisham and Greenwich NHS Trust

Role:
- `Advanced Information Analyst`
- `Lewisham and Greenwich NHS Trust`
- `Performance and Business Intelligence`
- `Band 7`

Using the updated job-ad evidence doc, the ideal candidate profile, and the application-materials operating guide as source material, the recruiter's calls for this role are:

1. **Delivered senior-level business intelligence and performance analysis that supported operational, clinical, executive, and corporate decision-making.**

2. **Produced accurate, timely, high-quality information analysis that informed service delivery, service improvement, business planning, and performance management.**

3. **Analysed highly complex, multi-stranded datasets and turned them into meaningful intelligence rather than only producing extracts or static reports.**

4. **Extracted, linked, validated, and analysed large complex datasets using advanced technical information skills.**

5. **Used advanced `SQL` to write complex queries, link interrelated data models, and produce reliable analytical datasets.**

6. **Used `Python` and/or `R` for forecasting, predictive modelling, statistical analysis, simulation, optimisation, or advanced modelling.**

7. **Delivered demand and capacity modelling that supported business planning, service improvement, productivity analysis, and efficiency decisions.**

8. **Produced productivity, efficiency, activity, and performance analysis across operational domains such as elective care, outpatients, diagnostics, or comparable service areas.**

9. **Built, maintained, improved, or replaced reporting solutions, dashboards, KPIs, and monitoring mechanisms using tools such as `Power BI`, `SSRS`, `Excel`, `Python`, or similar BI tooling.**

10. **Automated recurring reports and analytical processes to improve efficiency, reduce manual work, and strengthen consistency across the analyst team.**

11. **Developed and documented procedures, reporting logic, definitions, validation steps, and standard operating processes so outputs remained repeatable and service-continuity safe.**

12. **Applied robust data-quality, validation, reconciliation, and assurance processes before using information for decision-making.**

13. **Worked within sensitive-information and governance expectations, including `Data Protection`, information governance, confidentiality, `GDPR`, `Caldicott`, records management, and `FOI` awareness.**

14. **Produced interpretative narratives, deep dives, benchmarking analysis, and pressure-point intelligence that explained not only what changed but why it mattered operationally.**

15. **Supported performance monitoring, activity planning, contract planning, KPI development, and local or national monitoring requirements.**

16. **Translated complex information into clear, actionable messages for varied audiences, including non-technical managers, clinicians, senior managers, executives, analysts, and technical peer groups.**

17. **Worked with users to define reporting specifications, select suitable presentation formats, negotiate timescales, and manage expectations where analytical findings were sensitive, contentious, or challenged existing assumptions.**

18. **Operated independently in a fast-paced complex organisation with multiple priorities, prolonged concentration demands, tight deadlines, and changing requirements.**

19. **Led analytical work packages from specification to delivery and showed enough technical maturity to guide methods, quality, documentation, and outputs.**

20. **Shared knowledge, trained or supported colleagues, mentored less experienced analysts, and strengthened wider analytical capability through standards, documentation, or technical guidance.**

21. **Presented as a Band 7-level performance intelligence analyst: technically advanced, operationally aware, governance-conscious, improvement-focused, and able to produce trusted intelligence for high-stakes service decisions.**

The spine of the call here is:

- senior NHS performance intelligence and operational BI
- demand and capacity modelling, forecasting, simulation, optimisation, and statistical analysis
- productivity, efficiency, activity, and performance insight
- advanced `SQL`, `Python` / `R`, `Excel`, `Power BI`, and `SSRS`
- dashboard, KPI, reporting-solution, and monitoring-mechanism delivery
- data extraction, linking, validation, reconciliation, and assurance
- governance, confidentiality, `GDPR`, `Caldicott`, `FOI`, and sensitive-information handling
- automation, documentation, standard operating procedures, and service continuity
- deep-dive analysis, benchmarking, interpretative narrative, and pressure-point escalation
- stakeholder communication with operational, clinical, executive, corporate, and technical audiences
- independent delivery, competing-priority management, mentoring, training, and analytical leadership

Truth-boundary note:
- The candidate should not claim direct NHS employment, direct patient-level or clinical data handling, direct clinician or executive stakeholder management, direct `FOI` response ownership, direct `Caldicott` operational practice, direct `SSRS` ownership, direct line management, or direct Band 7 NHS performance leadership unless separately evidenced.
- The strongest transferable evidence should be translated from the governed fraud-risk analytical platform, COVID-19 policy/activity analysis, network-analysis project, MSc data-science work, engineering field experience, and live service work.
- The final application should sound like advanced performance intelligence, demand-pressure modelling, data-quality assurance, automation, and decision-support analytics, not generic data science, not a junior analyst CV, and not a false NHS insider profile.
