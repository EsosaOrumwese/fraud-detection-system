# Sainsbury's Analyst, Nectar360 Supplier Analytics — 3 Screens

This is still only the **job-ad interpretation stage**: business/problem screen, technical/capability screen, and working-style/stakeholder screen. No proof buckets, evidence ledger, scoring, or bullets yet.

# 1. Business / problem screen

This is a **client-facing supplier analytics role** inside **Nectar360**, not a pricing-only role, not a generic marketing analyst role, and not an internal BI reporting role.

The hiring manager wants someone who can produce grocery and GM analysis for **FMCG clients**, especially around:

```text
customer journeys
segmentation
ranging
health
campaign attribution / N360 Pollen model outputs
customer behaviour
supplier/client recommendations
```

The CV must therefore prove:

| Nectar360 business problem           | What the CV needs to show                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| FMCG / GM client analytics           | You can produce analysis that a supplier/client could use, not just internal dashboards.          |
| Customer journeys                    | You can analyse customer behaviour over steps, stages, lifecycle, actions or response history.    |
| Segmentation                         | You can build customer groups from behavioural, lifecycle or response variables.                  |
| Ranging / product health             | Ideally, product/SKU/category evidence. If absent, do not fake it.                                |
| Campaign attribution / model outputs | You can interpret model/scoring outputs, not just build models.                                   |
| Customer behaviour understanding     | You can explain which customer groups behave differently and why that matters.                    |
| Recommendations                      | You can turn SQL/BI analysis into reports, presentations or client-facing recommendation outputs. |

The strongest positioning phrase is:

```text
customer segmentation and supplier analytics for retail / FMCG decision support
```

Not:

```text
promotion response analytics only
```

Not:

```text
pricing and promotions
```

Not:

```text
operational reporting
```

Not:

```text
generic customer behaviour ML
```

This CV needs to feel like: **"I can analyse granular customer/transaction-style data, segment customers, interpret model outputs, build BI views, and produce supplier/client-ready recommendations."** The ad explicitly frames the role around FMCG/GM client analysis, customer journeys, segmentation, ranging, health, campaign attribution model outputs, and cross-team customer behaviour projects.
