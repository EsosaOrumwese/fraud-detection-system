## Job Ad

# Senior Data and Information Analyst- CYPMHS Provider Collaborative

The closing date is 26 May 2026.

## Job Summary

North London NHS Foundation Trust is recruiting a Senior Data & Information Analyst to join the Children & Young People's Mental Health Services (CYPMHS) Provider Collaborative.

This is a key role within the Commissioning Hub, responsible for producing high-quality business intelligence that drives decision-making, strengthens performance, and supports system-wide transformation.

The postholder will combine technical expertise with operational understanding to develop dashboards, analyse performance, assure data quality, and provide clear, actionable insights to senior leaders, clinicians, and partners across the collaborative.

The role is positioned as an opportunity to influence how services are planned, monitored and improved for children and young people across the region.

## Main Duties Of The Job

- Developing high-quality dashboards, reports and analytics using Power BI and other BI tools.
- Providing expert analysis to support commissioning, performance management and service improvement.
- Leading on data quality assurance, validation and troubleshooting across multiple providers.
- Using statistical techniques, including SPC charts, to identify trends, variation and areas requiring recovery.
- Producing clear, accessible narratives for non-technical audiences.
- Supporting the planning cycle and responding to routine and ad-hoc information requests.
- Working closely with the Senior BI Analyst to enhance BI functionality and reporting capability.
- Contributing to the continuous improvement of information systems, processes and reporting frameworks.
- Providing administrative support to the senior management team, including minute-taking and preparation of papers.

## About The Trust

The partnership between Barnet, Enfield and Haringey Mental Health NHS Trust (BEH) and Camden and Islington NHS Foundation Trust (C&I) has been developing since 2021 as the North London Mental Health Partnership.

To meet the needs of Partnership services, staff may be required from time to time to work at different locations under Barnet, Enfield and Haringey Trust and Camden and Islington Trust.

## Job Purpose

To support the analytical function of the Commissioning Hub by delivering high-quality, accurate, and meaningful business intelligence that informs decision-making and reflects pathway and provider performance.

The role combines operational understanding with analytical and technical expertise to develop dashboards, reports, and data insights, ensure data quality and assurance, and provide information support to key business areas in response to routine and ad-hoc requirements.

## Main Responsibilities

The postholder will:

- Support the analytical function of the Commissioning Hub and the development and presentation of accurate and meaningful business intelligence reflecting all aspects of pathway performance.
- Combine operational awareness and understanding with knowledge of data and systems.
- Develop dashboards and data analytics for a broad range of contractual metrics.
- Provide an information assurance function, ensuring that data is of a high quality and using BI tools to create high-quality reports and dashboards.
- Assist with preparation, analysis and interpretation of ad-hoc information.
- Provide information support to key business areas, including supporting the planning process and assisting with the development and provision of dashboards.
- Ensure data quality, consistency and accuracy is of the required standard through regular checking and data validation.
- Contribute to the continuous development and efficient production of information reports and service analysis to meet the developing needs of the provider collaborative.

## Business Intelligence Responsibilities

- Proactively develop knowledge of the Commissioning Hub, provider collaborative, and the information and broader business intelligence needs of clinicians and managers.
- Take the initiative to identify data quality issues and underperforming areas, and work with relevant stakeholders to develop and implement recovery actions as appropriate.
- Pragmatically mitigate data quality challenges.
- Effectively review the quality of quantitative contract submissions and support the Partnership Manager to identify areas of improvement.
- Gather data extractions accurately and according to set timetables and reporting cycles.
- Use statistical process control (SPC) charts and other statistical techniques to identify when performance is deteriorating or below target and not improving, and track improvements in line with recovery plans.
- Aggregate information from multiple sources and across providers.
- Proactively identify key gaps in provider information, then work with colleagues to scope, specify and build new system extracts to better meet service information and business intelligence needs.
- Provide helpful and considered analysis to support Commissioning Hub priorities, making use of and linking information from a range of source systems.
- Undertake complex ad-hoc analysis in areas defined by senior management, including managing potentially contradictory and complex data to ascertain a credible, easily understandable position.
- Present analysis to internal audiences as necessary.
- Review guidance and identify ad-hoc queries that may support the Commissioning Hub's financial or performance position.
- Research the use of more advanced analytical techniques which deliver predictive and prescriptive analytics, and share these within the team to support continuous improvement.
- Take responsibility for delegated information streams within the department.

## Presentation And Reporting

- Communicate reports to non-statisticians, explaining complex detail in an easy-to-understand manner using a range of techniques.
- Provide requestors with a clear narrative including assumptions and data sources so they can interpret analyses or reports.
- Brief requestors in person as well as in writing.
- Collate and present routine activity monitoring information, dashboards and performance reports in a timely and accurate manner to agreed formats and timescales.
- Ensure clear interpretation has been made to assist providers.

## Information Management

- Ensure optimal use is made of Power BI to provide the management team with high-quality reports that bring together different sources of information into a comprehensive and integrated analysis of service performance.
- Act as a champion for Power BI across the Commissioning Hub.
- Support and encourage clinicians and managers to make best use of the system to access and build intelligent information reports that help monitor and improve service delivery and clinical outcomes.
- Liaise closely with the Senior Business Intelligence Analyst to consider new BI system functionality that may support the directorate's developing information requirements.

## Data Quality And Information Governance

- Monitor the quality of data and work with providers to improve the quality of data, negotiating as required.
- Observe utmost rigour in protecting patient-identifiable data, including continual improvement of filing practices and security techniques.
- Ensure all reports meet specified validation and quality control requirements before publication.
- Ensure new reporting protocols, procedures and timetables are documented, and existing protocols, procedures and timetables are updated as appropriate to support business continuity.
- Resolve routine queries relating to developed reports and escalate system-related issues.
- Take appropriate action to resolve anomalies found within extracted data or reports, seeking support where appropriate.

## Admin Support

- Take and transcribe minutes at meetings.
- Provide clerical and administrative support to the CYPMHS PC senior management team and Administration Manager.
- Attend meetings and produce timely, accurate agendas, minutes, presentations and reports for Trust-level dissemination.
- Work to a very high standard with strong attention to detail.
