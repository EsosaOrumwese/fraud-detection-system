I come from a mixed operations and technical background.

I started in field engineering, working around completions equipment, yard checks, loadouts, and documentation. That gave me my first exposure to operational discipline: making sure the right equipment was ready, the paperwork matched, and small assumptions did not become bigger problems later.

After that, I came to the UK for my MSc Data Science at the University of Exeter. That strengthened the analytical side of how I work: breaking messy problems into steps, using data to understand what is actually happening, and explaining results clearly.

Since finishing my MSc, I have been working at Five Guys, which has given me a direct frontline view of operations. I have seen how rushes work, how bottlenecks affect customers and the wider team, how prep and handover discipline matter, and how newer crew members sometimes need structure rather than just more pressure.

That is why Area Manager feels like a serious next step for me. I am interested in work where people, process, standards, and pressure all meet. I want to build on my operational background, analytical training, and frontline experience in a role where I can help teams understand what is blocked, protect the standard, and keep the operation moving under pressure.
