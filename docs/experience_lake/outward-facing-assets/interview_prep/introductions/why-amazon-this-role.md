Amazon interests me because the Area Manager role is practical leadership in a live operation.

What appeals to me is that it is not leadership from a distance. In a fulfilment centre, safety, quality, productivity, customer promises, and team performance are all moving at the same time. That fits the kind of operational work I am drawn to, where you have to understand what is happening on the floor, not just manage from reports.

I am also interested in the corrective-action side of the role. If performance drops, or a shift is not running to standard, the answer is not just to tell people to work harder. You have to look at the metrics, understand the root cause, support the team, and fix the process in a way that protects safety and quality.

So Amazon appeals to me because the role would stretch the things I already care about: leading people under pressure, using data properly, improving flow, and helping a team deliver consistently at pace.
