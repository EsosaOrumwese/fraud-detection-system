Once I saw that, I changed the setup. Containers, lids, labels, and bin position were ready before I started. I also asked faster crew members to show me their sequence, and I copied the parts that reduced extra movement.

Over the next two weeks, the same prep list came down from about 70 minutes to around 55 minutes. So the deeper issue was not simply my speed. It was invisible waste between tasks, and the evidence was the whiteboard timing plus the reduction in upstairs chasing.

My backup story for Dive Deep would be the **MSc classification sample** story.
